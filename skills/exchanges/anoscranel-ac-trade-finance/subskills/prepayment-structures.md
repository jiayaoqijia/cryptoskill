# Prepayment structures

## Standard

A prepayment is an advance against future delivery: the financier pays now, and the goods are delivered later against that payment. It is common in commodities where the producer needs capital and the buyer needs supply, and it converts a finance exposure into a performance exposure, because the financier's recovery depends on the producer delivering.

The risk is therefore not primarily price. It is whether the producer can produce, whether the offtake agreement obliges it to deliver to this buyer ahead of others, and what happens if production falls short. A prepayment secured on future production of an asset that has not been built is a claim on a project, and it should be analysed as one.

Primary sources: <https://www.bis.org/> and <https://www.icc-ccs.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Record the advance amount, the delivery schedule, and the pricing formula.
- Record the security: assignment of the offtake, a charge over assets, or a guarantee.
- Record the producer's capacity and the asset's stage of development.
- Record the priority of the offtake against other buyers and lenders.
- Record the shortfall mechanism: what happens if delivery is less than scheduled.
- Record the step-in rights and whether the financier can take over the offtake.

## Procedure

1. Record the advance and the delivery terms.
2. Record the security package.
3. Record production capacity and asset stage.
4. Record priority against other claims.
5. Record the shortfall mechanism.
6. Write the prepayment analysis with the performance risk named.

## Anti-patterns

- Analysing a prepayment as a price exposure.
- Ignoring the asset's stage of development.
- Assuming the offtake ranks ahead of other obligations.
- Overlooking the shortfall mechanism.
- Treating a guarantee as protection without assessing the guarantor.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Advance and schedule recorded.
- [ ] Security package recorded.
- [ ] Capacity and stage recorded.
- [ ] Priority recorded.
- [ ] Shortfall mechanism recorded.
- [ ] Analysis written.

The offtake's priority is the provision that determines recovery: a producer that has committed its output to several buyers, or pledged the same production to a lender, delivers to whoever ranks first, and a prepayment that sits behind other claims is an unsecured advance with a commodity narrative. Recording the ranking against every other claim on the same production is the core of the analysis.
