# Slide Decks

## Overview

The craft of making a slide deck shareable and readable, worked on as text and structure: the brief, the story spine, per-slide claims, chart choice, reading load, projection typography, exported-file accessibility, and recurring quality control. The reader is a general agent drafting or reviewing a deck's text and outline, not a binary editor.

## When to use

- Drafting, outlining, or restructuring a slide deck.
- Reviewing a deck someone else wrote, at the text level.
- Turning a recurring report into a reusable deck template.
- Choosing or challenging the charts inside a deck.

## When NOT to use

Talk scripts and speaker notes are outside this skill; ac-narrative-writing covers that craft. Document typesetting and the README/how-to/reference doc set are outside; ac-technical-writing covers those. Binary manipulation of a .pptx file is outside by design: this skill works on the deck as text, structure, and chart decisions.
