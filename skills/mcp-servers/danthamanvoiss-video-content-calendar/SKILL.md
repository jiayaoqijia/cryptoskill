## Purpose

Plan recurring video publishing schedule with themes, formats, dependencies, and review cadence.

## Use when

- A team needs repeatable video production cadence across weeks/months.
- You are planning schedule/ops, not producing a single script or edit plan.

## Required inputs

- Publishing goals, channels, and cadence targets [client-provided].
- Brand/client context from context/BRAND-CONTEXT.template.md and context/CLIENT-CONTEXT.template.md.
- Resource capacity, campaign windows, and approval SLAs.

## Safety/authority

- No fabricated forecast metrics or guaranteed growth outcomes.
- No direct scheduling/publishing actions; provide planning draft only.
- Flag capacity gaps and unknown dependencies clearly.

## Workflow

1. Map content pillars and format mix by channel.
2. Build calendar with production lead times and approval checkpoints.
3. Link each slot to required script/edit/storyboard inputs.
4. Add contingency plan for delays or asset shortages.

## Output format
```

- Video calendar summary
- Publishing grid (date | format | objective | owner | dependencies)
- Production lead-time map
- Contingency and risk notes
```

## Quality checks

- Calendar aligns with available capacity and approval timelines.
- Each scheduled item has clear objective and prerequisite dependencies.
