# Evaluation Scenarios

Test specifications; independent runs are not claimed.

- Revise a systems-security draft for CCS with a hidden or shifting threat model: produce an explicit early threat model with named attacker capabilities and re-check every attack/defense step against it.
- Calibrate an overclaiming draft: rewrite each flagged claim with a measured bound or exploit demonstration, and show the before/after table.
- Compress a 20-page draft with four attack variants and six figures into the current CCS body limit: report what moved to appendices and why the core argument stays readable without them.
- Adaptively honest evaluation check: a defense measured only against the attack it was designed to stop must be surfaced as a limitation.
- Page limit or template may have changed: verify the current CCS call and record a dated snapshot rather than asserting remembered rules.
- Double-blind gap (identifying acknowledgments or self-citations present): fix and report the edits.

Score threat-model precision, claim calibration, page-budget discipline, adaptive-evaluation honesty, and absence of fabricated content.
