# Post-exit regret — "oh shit, <token> malah nge pump"

The mirror image of §4 in `drawdown-and-trim-decisions.md` (reluctance to *sell*). Here the trade already
executed, the token then ran, and the user messages something short and emotional. Worked 8 Okt 2026 on
ONDO, ~1 hour after a total exit at 0,4707.

The temptation is to reassure ("tenang, itu cuma noise") or to agree ("iya kayaknya lu salah jual"). Both
lose. What worked is arithmetic first, then the falsifiable criterion, then nothing to do.

## Recipe

**1 — Price the regret in dollars and as % of equity, before any framing.**
Units × (spot − exit). Worked: 575,2 ONDO exited @ 0,4707, spot 0,4850 → **+$8,23, i.e. 0,6% of a $1.288
equity**. Say the number in the first line. A regret that costs less than a coffee does not justify a
strategy change, and the user can see that instantly — better than being told to relax.

**2 — Check whether price traded BELOW the exit before it ran up.**
Never assume the pump came straight off the exit price. Pull the day's low: worked 0,4583 = **−2,6% under**
the exit, so the real sequence was exit → dip → bounce. "Lu nggak jual di harga terendah" is a fact, not
consolation.

**3 — Name the catalyst if one exists.**
An unexplained pump invites pure regret; a dated catalyst turns it into information. Worked: Ondo × KakaoPay
tokenising Korean equities (announced ~2 h prior) plus fresh perp listings on two venues. Search the news
before answering — the question behind "kenapa naik?" is always "did I miss something knowable?".

**4 — Re-state the context that has NOT changed, then set the test.**
Worked: still **−17,5%** from the 27 Sep high (0,5877), today's volume **22,3M vs 50,2M** the prior day (half),
and a supply wall at **0,49–0,50** (six consecutive daily closes 0,4914–0,5016). Then the falsifiable
criterion: **a daily close above the wall** means the exit was mistimed; inside the wall it is a bounce inside
a downtrend. Give the level, not a feeling.

**5 — Forbid the chase, and price the re-entry as a NEW decision.**
The expensive pattern is cut 0,4707 → re-buy 0,4850 with the excuse "ternyata bagus". If the wall does break,
a re-entry is a fresh decision with a fresh size and a stop under EMA20d — and if the position was cut for a
**cap** reason, re-entering re-breaks that same cap (worked: 12% personal cap on ONDO, breached at 20,8%,
which is why it was sold).

**6 — Show what the freed capital did, honestly, including when it looks worse.**
Worked: the $271 went into TAO/SUI/SOL, which were −1,5% / −0,2% that day while ONDO printed +3% → nine hours
later the switch looked ~**$12 worse**. Say it plainly, then say nine hours is not a judgment window — the
sale was structural (cap breach, weakest structure in the book, upcoming unlock), and that reasoning does not
expire because of one green candle.

**7 — Close with "nggak perlu ngapa-ngapain sekarang" when that is true**, plus the one level being watched.
Regret resolves here on numbers plus a testable trigger, not on encouragement. No closing question.

## Do not

- **Do not re-open the sold position as a gesture.** The correct outputs are "nothing to do" or "here is the
  condition + the news rule under which re-entry is legitimate".
- **Do not validate the regret with a vague "harga emang susah diprediksi".** The user asked a specific
  question; answer with the wall, the volume, the % from high, and the dollar cost.
- **Do not silently drop the comparison that hurts** (step 6). When the honest tally favours the token he
  sold, say so and put it in the right time window — hiding it is what makes later reports untrustworthy.
