# Portfolio Review

## Standard

The periodic portfolio review is a structured governance process designed to evaluate strategy performance, verify risk budget adherence, audit correlation assumptions, and execute strategy sunsetting when necessary. In crypto markets, rapid structural shifts render static strategies obsolete; regular reviews ensure that capital is reallocated away from degraded models and toward robust frameworks that conform to current macroeconomic and liquidity realities. Systematic reviews prevent emotional decision-making, enforce accountability across all trading operations, and maintain alignment with overarching risk mandates. Evaluating historical performance against pre-established benchmarks ensures continuous adaptation to evolving market structures and liquidity cycles across multiple operating venues. Maintaining rigorous review standards guarantees long-term operational viability and capital protection across all active trading accounts.

Primary sources for the claims here: <https://www.cfainstitute.org/> and <https://www.iosco.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Portfolio reviews must occur at fixed calendar intervals (such as monthly or quarterly) and immediately following any major market stress event or systemic shock.
- Performance attribution analysis must isolate true strategy alpha from general market beta exposure and broad digital asset price appreciation.
- Any strategy that breaches drawdown limits or fails its pre-registered performance criteria for two consecutive review cycles must be systematically sunsetted.
- Correlation matrices, tail risk assumptions, and concentration registers must be recalculated and audited during every scheduled review cycle.

## Procedure

1. Compile comprehensive trade logs, PnL statements, and risk metric reports for all active trading strategies across the entire review period.
2. Perform rigorous performance attribution analysis to separate skill-based alpha returns from directional market beta and price movement.
3. Review actual risk utilization, volatility metrics, and drawdown statistics against pre-committed portfolio risk budgets and limits.
4. Update correlation matrices, tail dependence parameters, and concentration registers using recent market and liquidity data.
5. Execute termination, capital re-allocation, or restructuring protocols for underperforming or structurally compromised trading strategies.

## Anti-patterns

- Conducting formal portfolio reviews only when a catastrophic loss occurs rather than adhering to a strict calendar schedule.
- Attributing broad market beta appreciation entirely to individual strategy execution skill without isolation testing.
- Maintaining underperforming or structurally broken strategies in the portfolio due to psychological sunk-cost bias.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Periodic review schedule is established, documented, and strictly followed across operations.
- [ ] Performance attribution successfully separates true strategy alpha from broad market beta.
- [ ] Strategy sunsetting and capital re-allocation rules are enforced based on review findings.
