## Purpose
Provide affiliates with approved messaging and referral workflows without unauthorized claims.

## Use when
- Onboarding a new affiliate/referral partner.
- Refreshing partner messaging and handoff flow.

## Required inputs
- Approved offer and brand claims.
- Referral qualification criteria.
- Partner channel and audience profile.

## Safety/authority
- Draft-only by default; no automated outreach or system writes.
- No unapproved claims, guarantees, or fabricated proof.
- Include brand do/don’t rules and escalation path.

## Workflow
1. Summarize partner role and referral journey.
2. Draft approved positioning and talk tracks.
3. Define lead handoff criteria and SLA expectations.
4. Create compliance checklist for partner use.

## Output format
```
- Partner quickstart
- Approved messaging
- Referral process
- Compliance checklist
- Escalation contacts
```

## Quality checks
- Messaging uses approved claim set only.
- Referral criteria are explicit and actionable.
