## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may act on incorrect network, chain ID, bridge, token contract, or gas-token guidance when preparing crypto transactions.

Mitigation: Review transaction details in the user's own wallet and official sources before signing, including network, chain ID, bridge, token contract, and gas token.

Risk: Bridge and token approval actions can expose users to smart-contract, wrong-network, or scam-token loss.

Mitigation: Prefer official sources, verify contract addresses, review token approvals, and avoid signing transactions until the destination network and gas requirements are clear.
