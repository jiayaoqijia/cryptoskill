# Conduct record

## Standard

The conduct record is the review's audit trail assembled as it runs: the date-stamped protocol, the search log, the screening decisions with their disagreements, the extraction forms by version, the bias assessments by domain, the live count sheet, and the amendment history. Its purpose is not compliance decoration; it is that a hostile reader can reconstruct every decision and find either that it followed the protocol or that it was recorded as a departure.

This record is what separates conduct that was systematic from conduct that is described as systematic. The description lives in the final report, and reports are written with the benefit of hindsight; the record was made when the decisions were made. When the two disagree, the record wins, and a review that cannot produce one has only its report.

Primary sources: <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook> and <https://osf.io/prereg/>.

## Decision rules

- The record holds the protocol with its version history and dates.
- The search log, screening decisions, and disagreement resolutions are included with their dates.
- Extraction forms are archived per version with their pilot evidence.
- Bias assessments are archived per study and per domain, with supporting text.
- Every amendment carries the date, the change, the reason, and what prompted it.
- The record is assembled as the review runs, not reconstructed at the end.

## Procedure

1. Open the record at protocol registration and add the registration identifier.
2. File each stage's artifacts as it completes: searches, screens, forms, assessments.
3. Date everything; the chronology is part of the evidence.
4. Log amendments as they occur, not in a batch before submission.
5. Before write-up, walk the record end-to-end and reconcile it with the report's method section.
6. Retain the record with the review, and reference it from the report.

## Anti-patterns

- A record reconstructed at submission from memory and email.
- Only the final versions of forms kept, so piloting is unverifiable.
- Disagreements recorded as resolved without the resolution reason.
- The report's method section differing from the record with no explanation.
- Amendments batched to look planned.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Protocol and versions in the record.
- [ ] Search log and screening decisions filed with dates.
- [ ] Extraction forms archived per version.
- [ ] Bias assessments archived with supporting text.
- [ ] Amendments logged as they occurred.
- [ ] Record reconciled with the report before submission.
