## Description:

Think about digital assets like an institutional allocator, not a retail speculator; analyze custody, risk isolation, exposure design, recordkeeping, and tax-aware operational discipline for long-term crypto asset management.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users such as high-net-worth crypto holders, family-office-style operators, and treasury-minded digital asset allocators use this skill to assess custody, concentration, recordkeeping, and tax-awareness risks in long-term digital-asset operations.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat governance guidance as legal, tax, investment, compliance, or custody due diligence advice.

Mitigation: Keep outputs framed as operating discipline, require specialist review for jurisdiction-specific legal, tax, compliance, or custody decisions, and avoid trade or transfer recommendations.

Risk: Users may share private keys, seed phrases, account credentials, or unnecessary identifying details while describing crypto operations.

Mitigation: Tell users not to share secrets or credentials and work from non-sensitive summaries of their custody, venue, and recordkeeping setup.

Risk: Assessments may be misleading when portfolio purpose, custody locations, liquidity needs, recordkeeping habits, or jurisdictional sensitivity are missing.

Mitigation: Ask for missing context before presenting a governance assessment and state uncertainty where the provided facts are incomplete.
