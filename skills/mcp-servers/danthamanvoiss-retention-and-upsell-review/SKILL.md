## Purpose
Assess renewal risk and expansion opportunities with client-safe, evidence-led recommendations.

## Use when
- Pre-renewal planning or risk review.
- Identifying expansion paths without overpromising.

## Required inputs
- Client goals, current scope, and outcomes data.
- Stakeholder sentiment/feedback (if provided).
- Known budget/timeline constraints.

## Safety/authority
- Recommendations are hypotheses until validated with client data.
- No coercive or misleading upsell tactics.
- No guaranteed outcomes.

## Workflow
1. Score account health drivers.
2. Identify retention risks and mitigation actions.
3. Generate expansion hypotheses tied to observed value.
4. Prepare client-safe recommendation summary.

## Output format
```
- Health snapshot
- Risk register
- Expansion hypotheses
- Recommended next conversation points
```

## Quality checks
- Expansion suggestions tie to demonstrated needs/value.
- Risks and unknowns are explicit.
