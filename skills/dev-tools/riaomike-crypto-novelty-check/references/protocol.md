# Adversarial Novelty Protocol

## Decompose novelty dimensions

| ID | Construction/object | Property/security | Model/setup | Assumption | Parameters | Technique | Claimed improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Test each dimension and combination. “New” may mean functionality, model, assumption, proof method, parameter regime, asymptotic result, concrete result, implementation, or application; do not merge these categories.

## Search to disconfirm

- current, historical, acronym, expanded, and alternative terminology;
- stronger/weaker security notions and neighboring models;
- ring/module/unstructured variants and equivalent formulations;
- precursor techniques, surveys/theses/standards as maps, and cited predecessors;
- backward/forward citations, key authors/venues, corrections, attacks, and negative results;
- adjacent fields and included patent/non-English/unpublished sources.

Log only executed searches. Verify full sources before claim comparison.

## Closest-work matrix

| Work/version | Matching claim IDs | Formal object | Model/security | Assumptions | Parameters | Technique/cost | Match classification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classifications: material match, partial overlap, enabling predecessor, independent concurrent work, non-comparable, or false positive.

## Verdict grades

- **Matching prior art found:** exact source/version/location materially matches.
- **Partial prior-art map:** relevant work exists but coverage/verification gaps remain.
- **Strong bounded negative search:** documented multi-source, synonym, version, and citation search reaches a stated stopping rule without a match.
- **Not assessable:** claim or evidence boundary is insufficient.

## Allowed wording

“No earlier result matching X was found in sources Y, queries Z, through date D under stopping rule R; closest works A/B differ in M/N. Coverage excludes G. This is not proof of global novelty.”
