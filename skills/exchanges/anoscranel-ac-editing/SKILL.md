# Editing

## Overview

Six leaves cover everything after the first draft exists. Two passes change meaning: structural revision, then line editing. Two do not: copyedit against the style sheet, then the proofread keyed to the final artifact. Feedback-editing covers commenting on another's draft, and ai-assisted-writing covers where AI may touch text and what each venue demands disclosed.

## When to use

- Restructuring a draft, reordering or cutting sections, re-settling the argument.
- Sentence-level tightening that keeps meaning fixed.
- The style-sheet consistency pass, then the final error hunt and publish gate.
- Giving editorial notes on someone else's text.
- Working out AI-use policy for the venue, and the disclosure statement.

## When not to use

- Writing the first draft from notes — outside this skill.
- Building the style sheet or picking the base guide — outside this skill.
- Checking sources, resolving citations, or plagiarism review — outside this skill.
