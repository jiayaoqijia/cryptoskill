# Evidence-First Paper Protocol

## Freeze the ledger

| Claim ID | Exact claim | Type | Evidence/location | Status | Allowed wording | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

No contribution sentence exists without a ledger row.

## Architecture

1. **Problem and stakes:** technically precise, not hype.
2. **Barrier/gap:** verified against closest work.
3. **Insight/construction:** what is genuinely new or differently assembled.
4. **Claims:** functionality, correctness, security, complexity, empirical, artifact.
5. **Evidence:** theorem/proof/reduction, parameters, experiment, source.
6. **Limits:** model, setup, assumptions, parameters, failure, deployment, ethics.

## Section gates

- **Title/abstract:** no unsupported universals; all numbers/claims appear in evidence.
- **Introduction:** contribution list is atomic and body-addressable; closest work is fair.
- **Preliminaries:** only needed definitions; exact notation/security games/assumptions.
- **Construction:** complete algorithms/interfaces and correctness conditions.
- **Security:** theorem statements, proof status, reduction loss, model/setup/adversary, composition.
- **Parameters/experiments:** reproducible config, compatible baselines, statistics, negative results.
- **Related work:** taxonomy and claim-level comparison, not citation dumping.
- **Limitations/ethics/open science:** explicit and venue-compliant.

## Cryptography writing rules

Use “proves” only for a complete checked argument in the stated model; “claims” for author assertions; “supports/suggests” for limited empirical evidence; “not verified” for missing evidence. Distinguish soundness/knowledge/ZK and LWE variants. Define every parameter and cost regime.

## Final audit

Check notation/definitions, cross-references, theorem hypotheses, proof status, parameter values, failure accumulation, table/figure provenance, citation identity/support, abstract/body consistency, novelty wording, anonymity, ethics/artifacts, and current venue rules.
