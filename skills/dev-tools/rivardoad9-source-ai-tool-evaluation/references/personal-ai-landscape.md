# Personal AI assistants — competitive map

Snapshot read late Oct 2026 from TechCrunch, YC pages, vendor docs and pricing. Treat every number as
perishable: re-verify the specific figure before quoting it, and say when it was read.

## The layer that decides the category: platform owners

Meta (Muse), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Apple (Siri), Google (Gemini). They own the messaging surface and the account.
They do not compete on features, they ship one and the category resets. Any "will people use this?" answer
that ignores this layer is wrong.

## Funded independents

- **Instinct** — raised ~$1B at a ~$10B valuation (late Sep 2026). Founder Noah Shinn, known for SWE-bench.
  Access is invite-only waitlist, free while in it. Highest capital in the category; access not yet a promise.
- **Tab** — emerged from stealth at ~$300M valuation (Oct 2026). Newest, least evidence.
- **Poke** (Interaction Company) — acquired by Cognition (Jul 2026), reported low nine figures. Acquisition
  means the consumer roadmap is no longer the product's own; treat as wait-and-see.
- **Martin** — $21–49/month, can place calls and book on the user's behalf. Real actions, real price.
- **Town** — work-flavoured, email-native. Wrong shape for personal life admin.

## Free / open / niche

- **folk** (Nozomio Inc, San Francisco, YC Summer 2025) — founder Arlan Rakhmetzhanov, dropped out in grade 11
  in Kazakhstan, youngest solo founder from the region in YC. Seed ~$6.2M led by CRV, with BoxGroup, LocalGlobe,
  YC, 20VC; angels include Paul Graham, Paul Buchheit (Gmail), Gokul Rajaram. Product ~3 months old at review
  time. Lives in WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, Discord. Connects Gmail, calendar, bank via Plaid read-only;
  Google read-only option exists. Free tier advertised at 100 messages/day; pricing page advertises Pro $9.99
  and a Max tier while docs state new paid subscriptions are not currently offered.
- **Pally** — free, claims near-unlimited usage, works inside iMessage, WhatsApp and Telegram, reads and replies
  in chats. Cheapest way to test the category; expect pricing to arrive.
- **Comma** — open source, free, self-hosted, runs on Signal/Telegram/WeChat with computer and file access.
  Most capability and most setup work; the only option where the user can read the whole pipeline.
- **Caddy** — iMessage/RCS, public beta since Apr 2026.
- **Ask Proxy** — $1/day over SMS, narrow positioning.
- **Fambot** — family "chief of staff" framing.

## How to read this map

Three sentences, in this order:

1. The category is real and funded, so "is this a thing?" is settled; the open question is who survives.
2. Money per player spans three orders of magnitude ($6M seed vs $1B raise), so "which vendor is durable" has a
   factual answer, not a taste answer.
3. The realistic pick for a person who wants research plus reminders today is the free tier of one of the
   small players, with bank and email left unlinked. The paid-for-action products are for people who want
   bookings and calls, not notes.

## Naming traps

- **folk.com** (Nozomio, personal AI) vs **folk.app** (Paris CRM): separate companies. Trustpilot and G2 reviews
  for folk.app describe a sales CRM, and reading them as folk.com evidence gets the verdict backwards.
- Several assistants ship under single common words; confirm the domain on every review page before citing it.
