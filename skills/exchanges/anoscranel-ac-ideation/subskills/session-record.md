# Session Record

## Standard

- **Farnam Street, decision journal**: recording the decision, the reasoning, and the expectation at the time lets later review separate what you knew from what happened. <https://fs.blog/decision-journal/>
- **Delbecq & Van de Ven (1971)**: the nominal group output is an ordered list plus a record of who ranked what; a session with no record is not a result. <https://doi.org/10.1177/002188637100700404>
- A record states facts (ideas generated, scores) and decisions separately; an unattributed claim is neither.

## Decision rules

1. The idea count is the number actually generated, not a rounded or inflated figure.
2. A decision is recorded only with its owner; an unattributed claim goes in open questions, not decisions.
3. If the next test has no owner or date, the session did not finish; fill it in before closing.
4. Clusters and the shortlist are recorded as produced, not cleaned up after the fact.
5. The record is written at the close of the session, not reconstructed days later.

## Procedure

1. Record the question and the brief verbatim.
2. Record the constraints, each marked wall or fence.
3. Record the honest idea count and the raw list, or a pointer to it.
4. Record the clusters and the shortlist with their scores.
5. Record the decisions: what was chosen, and by whom.
6. Record the open questions, each attributed to its asker or marked unattributed.
7. Record the next test: what it is, who owns it, and the date it runs.

## Anti-patterns

- **Inflated count.** "We had about fifty ideas" when the list shows twelve. Fix: count the list.
- **Opinion as decision.** "We should pivot" written as if decided. Fix: attribute it, or demote it to an open question.
- **No owner, no date.** A next test that nobody owns and nobody scheduled. Fix: name both.
- **Reconstructed record.** Written from memory days later. Fix: write at the close.
- **Record without scores.** A shortlist with no reason each item made it. Fix: include the scores.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Question and brief are recorded verbatim.
- [ ] Constraints are recorded and marked wall or fence.
- [ ] The idea count is honest and matches the raw list.
- [ ] The shortlist carries scores.
- [ ] Decisions are attributed; open questions are listed.
- [ ] The next test has an owner and a date.
