# Tax pack assembly

## Standard

A tax pack is a handoff document. Its reader is a professional who has an hour, not a week, and who will be judged on what the pack omits as much as on what it contains. The pack therefore leads with the summary, follows with the reconciled detail, and ends with the open questions — in that order, because the professional decides where to spend attention from the summary.

The pack's structure is stable across years, so that a comparison is possible. Each year's pack carries the same sections, the same file naming, and the same reconciliation versioning. A pack whose structure changes annually forces the professional to rebuild the picture every year, which is both expensive and error-prone.

Primary sources: <https://www.irs.gov/> and <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing>.

## Decision rules

- The pack opens with a one-page summary: period, accounts, instruments, totals, and open questions.
- Reconciled detail follows, with the method and the version recorded.
- Venue statements are included as received, not re-typed.
- The handoff note lists what was done, what was assumed, and what was left open.
- File naming is stable across years and includes the period and the version.
- The pack is delivered with a manifest, so nothing is silently missing.

## Procedure

1. Write the one-page summary.
2. Assemble the reconciled detail with method and version.
3. Attach venue statements as received.
4. Write the handoff note with assumptions and open questions.
5. Build the manifest and check every listed item exists.
6. Date the pack and record its location.

## Anti-patterns

- Delivering raw exports with no summary.
- Re-typing statements instead of attaching them.
- Omitting the assumptions from the handoff note.
- Changing the structure every year.
- Sending a pack without a manifest.

## Done checklist

- [ ] One-page summary written.
- [ ] Reconciled detail with method and version.
- [ ] Statements attached as received.
- [ ] Handoff note with assumptions and open questions.
- [ ] Manifest built and checked.
- [ ] Pack dated and located.

The one-page summary is the highest-leverage page in the pack. It tells the professional where the risk is, which questions are open, and which accounts matter, in a minute. Packs that skip it are read in the order they were assembled, which is rarely the order of importance.
