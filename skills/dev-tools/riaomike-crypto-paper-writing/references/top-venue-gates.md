# Top-Venue Quality Gates

Policies change. Verify the target venue's current official call, author instructions, anonymity, ethics, AI, artifact, formatting, and submission rules at execution time.

## Research gates

- precise problem, significance, threat/security model, and contribution delta;
- atomic claims linked to proofs, experiments, artifacts, or verified sources;
- closest-work comparison with bounded novelty;
- exact assumptions, parameters, reduction losses, failure probabilities, and cost models;
- limitations and negative results visible rather than hidden;
- reproducible tables/figures and claim-to-artifact map;
- ethics, stakeholders/harms, privacy/consent, responsible disclosure, conflicts, and dual-use analysis;
- correct anonymity and human author accountability for AI-assisted content.

## Current official policy examples consulted

- [USENIX Security 2026 Call for Papers](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/call-for-papers): ethics and Open Science appendices, artifact access/justification, anonymity, and author responsibility.
- [USENIX Security 2026 Call for Artifacts](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/call-for-artifacts): availability plus optional functionality/reproducibility assessment.
- [ACM CCS 2026 Call for Papers](https://www.sigsac.org/ccs/CCS2026/call-for/call-for-papers.html): Open Science appendix, claim-dependent artifacts, anonymity, ethics, and human responsibility for AI-assisted content.
- [IEEE S&P 2026 Call for Papers](https://sp2026.ieee-security.org/cfpapers.html): anonymity, artifacts including theoretical proof supplements, ethics/disclosure, and content accuracy/originality.
- [IEEE S&P 2026 Artifact Instructions](https://sp2026.ieee-security.org/artifact_instructions.html): availability, functionality, reproduced results, claim mapping, portable packaging, and permanent access.
- [IACR policies and guidelines](https://www.iacr.org/docs/): current author/reviewer, ethics, conflict, and publication policies.

These examples do not replace the target venue's current rules and do not imply that every venue has identical requirements.
