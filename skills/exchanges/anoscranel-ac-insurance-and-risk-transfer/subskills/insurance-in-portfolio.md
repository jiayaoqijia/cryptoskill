# Insurance in the portfolio

## Standard

Insurance and the portfolio solve the same problem — funding a loss — through different mechanisms. A portfolio funds losses by selling assets, which works until the loss is large or arrives with a market fall; insurance funds them by paying a claim, at a premium cost. Coordinating the two means deciding which losses are funded from the portfolio's reserve and which are transferred.

The interaction is most visible at the edges: the emergency reserve should be sized against the deductibles and the retained risks, not independently; life and disability coverage should be aligned with the income they replace and the liabilities they cover; and liability coverage should be sized against the assets it protects, which grow with the portfolio.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://www.naic.org/>.

## Decision rules

- The reserve is sized against deductibles and retained risks, and the sizing is recorded.
- Life and disability coverage are recorded against the income and liabilities they replace.
- Liability limits are recorded against the assets they protect.
- Coverage that duplicates a portfolio function is recorded and justified, or removed.
- Changes to the portfolio that change the liability exposure trigger a coverage review.
- The coverage record and the investment policy statement reference each other.

## Procedure

1. Map each retained risk to the reserve that funds it.
2. Map each transferred risk to the coverage that addresses it.
3. Check for gaps and for duplication between the two.
4. Record the liability exposure against the asset base.
5. Set the trigger that reopens coverage when the portfolio changes materially.
6. Cross-reference the coverage record and the investment policy.

## Anti-patterns

- Sizing the reserve without reference to deductibles.
- Buying coverage that duplicates a portfolio function.
- Leaving liability limits unchanged as assets grow.
- Treating insurance and investment as separate decisions.
- No trigger for revisiting coverage.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Retained risks mapped to the reserve.
- [ ] Transferred risks mapped to coverage.
- [ ] Gaps and duplication recorded.
- [ ] Liability exposure against assets recorded.
- [ ] Review trigger set.
- [ ] Cross-references in place.
