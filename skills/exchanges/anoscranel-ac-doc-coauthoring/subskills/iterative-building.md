# Iterative Building

## Standard

Stage 2 executes document construction through disciplined, section-by-section iteration rather than monolithic dumps. The co-authoring loop requires drafting exactly one section at a time, engaging in brief brainstorming, refining prose, confirming accuracy with the author, and locking the section before moving forward. The standard prioritizes preserving the author's authentic voice over imposing standardized artificial intelligence phrasing. Disagreements are governed by strict authority boundaries: the author retains absolute ownership over substantive content, business logic, and strategic decisions, while the agent provides objective craft advice, structure tuning, and clarity edits that are flagged and explained rather than forced.

## Decision rules

1. Build the document strictly one section at a time, never generating multiple unverified sections simultaneously.
2. Preserve the author's core terminology, tone, and stylistic preferences rather than overwriting text with generic prose.
3. Capture key decisions, trade-offs, and rationale immediately inline or in decision logs as each section is confirmed.
4. When stylistic or structural disagreements occur, present craft recommendations clearly but yield final authority to the author on substance.
5. If the author requests skipping a section, record the omission status and allow continuation without blocking the flow.

## Procedure

1. Present the first planned section outline and invite brief bullet points or source notes from the author.
2. Draft the section prose based on gathered context and author input, applying plain language principles.
3. Review the drafted section with the author, highlighting key claims and checking against the success test.
4. Refine wording based on feedback, resolve any craft disagreements, and lock the section as confirmed.
5. Advance to the subsequent section in the skeleton and repeat the cycle until all planned sections are completed.

## Anti-patterns

- Generating an entire multi-page document in a single unreviewed output block.
- Replacing the author's distinct professional voice with repetitive, formulaic artificial intelligence filler.
- Ignoring author corrections on domain facts or arguing over substantive business decisions.
- Leaving completed sections unlocked so that subsequent revisions destabilize verified text.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Sections drafted sequentially one by one with active author collaboration.
- [ ] Author voice and terminology preserved throughout the text.
- [ ] Constructive craft advice offered without overriding author content authority.
- [ ] All sections confirmed, locked, and prepared for final reader testing.
