# Liquidity and vehicles

## Standard

The same bond exposure behaves differently depending on how it is held. Direct bonds mature and return principal; funds and ETFs do not, and their prices are set by the market's view of the underlying holdings. In calm markets the difference is small; in stress it is the whole story, because a vehicle that promises daily liquidity while holding instruments that trade weekly is a liquidity mismatch.

The mismatch is not a flaw in every case; it is a feature whose cost must be understood. An ETF can trade at a discount to its net asset value when the underlying market is illiquid, and a fund can gate redemptions. Both are documented possibilities and both have occurred in stress episodes.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://www.sec.gov/investor>.

## Decision rules

- Record the vehicle, its liquidity promise, and the liquidity of its underlying holdings.
- Where the two differ, state the mismatch and its historical cost in stress.
- Fund and ETF prices are compared with net asset value; a persistent premium or discount is a finding.
- Direct bonds are recorded with their maturity and the fact that principal returns at maturity if the issuer pays.
- Concentration in a single issuer or a single sector is recorded for funds as well as direct holdings.
- Redemption terms, gates, and fees are recorded, not assumed.

## Procedure

1. Identify the vehicle and its stated liquidity terms.
2. Identify the liquidity of the underlying holdings.
3. State the mismatch, or the absence of one.
4. Check the vehicle's premium or discount history.
5. Record concentration and redemption terms.
6. Record the comparison with a direct-holding alternative.

## Anti-patterns

- Assuming an ETF's daily trading means the underlying is liquid.
- Ignoring gates and fees until they are invoked.
- Comparing a fund's yield with a bond's yield without the maturity difference.
- Treating NAV as the price you will get in stress.
- Holding a single-issuer fund without recording the concentration.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Vehicle and liquidity terms identified.
- [ ] Underlying liquidity identified.
- [ ] Mismatch stated or absence noted.
- [ ] Premium/discount history checked.
- [ ] Concentration and redemption terms recorded.
- [ ] Direct-holding comparison recorded.
