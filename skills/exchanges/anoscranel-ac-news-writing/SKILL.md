# News Writing

## Overview

Two leaves cover public newswriting. journalism.md is the reporting side: the lede, the inverted pyramid, features, attribution and the legal line. press-release.md is the organisation's side: announcements, media statements, holding lines and boilerplate.

## When to use

- Writing a news story or feature, with structure, attribution and libel review.
- Writing a press release or media statement, with dateline and boilerplate.
- Preparing a holding line during a developing story.

## When not to use

- An internal memo or report read inside one organisation — outside this skill.
- Long-form narrative structure — outside this skill.
- Persuasive marketing copy — outside this skill.
