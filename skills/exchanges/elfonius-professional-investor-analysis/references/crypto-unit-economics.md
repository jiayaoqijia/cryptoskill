# Crypto Unit Economics — Revenue, Margin and Miner/Validator ROI

Purpose: test whether the business produces cash, not just activity. Token volume, TVL and emissions are not revenue.

## Revenue quality

- Paid versus incentive-farmed or related-party demand.
- External versus internal/group demand; disclose the related-party share.
- Recurring versus one-off; prepaid versus committed; spot versus contract.
- Revenue per product/model/segment; take rate; gross versus net revenue; incentives and subsidies.
- Customer concentration: top 1/5/10 share; related parties; refund/credit risk.
- Retention: cohort retention at 30/90/180 days; churn; net revenue retention.

## Unit-economics formulas

- Revenue per unit = revenue / units (users, GPUs, stores, contracts, tokens served).
- Cost per unit = GPU rental + power + bandwidth + operations + support.
- Gross margin = (revenue − variable cost) / revenue.
- Contribution margin = revenue − variable cost − incentives.
- CAC = sales and marketing / new customers; payback = CAC / monthly gross profit.
- Miner ROI = (emission value per day − cost per day) / hardware cost; payback days.
- Validator economics = stake × APR; take rate; root cut; delegation concentration.

## Tables

**Revenue bridge:** activity → paid activity → gross revenue → incentives → net revenue → gross profit.

**Unit economics:** segment, revenue/unit, cost/unit, gross margin, CAC/payback.

**Customer concentration:** top customers, share, contract type, related party, prepaid balance.

**Miner/validator:** count, top share, emission/day, cost/day, payback, take rate.

## Stress tests

- Price −20% / −50%; utilization −30%; emission −20%; GPU cost +30%; churn +50%.
- State which costs are fixed versus variable and which assumptions are judgmental.
- Show the break-even point: what revenue or utilization is needed to cover cash costs?

## Sources

- Issuer documents, epoch/provider dashboards, gateway billing, GPU rental benchmarks, customer disclosures, audited statements.
- Record the period, currency and whether revenue is gross or net.

## Red flags

- Revenue only from a related party or internal demand.
- Negative gross margin at current prices.
- Emissions greater than revenue; buyback funded by emissions or treasury, not revenue.
- Top customer >30% of revenue; prepaid refund risk; unearned revenue.
- Miner payback <30 days (mercenary capital) or >365 days (unviable).
- Revenue reported without definition, period or currency.

## Reporting

- Standard: one table plus a paragraph. Deep: full bridge, unit economics and stress tests.
- Label revenue as reported, estimated or claim; never present token volume as revenue.
- If revenue is undisclosed, say what would have to be true for the valuation to make sense; do not invent a number.
