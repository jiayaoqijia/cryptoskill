# Crypto Venue Paper Writing

Two defects drive this skill, and they are opposites. Places that must be **condensed** (the abstract, the contribution list) come out wordy; places that must be **detailed** (the technical overview, the concrete construction) come out thin. This skill decides both by number rather than by taste.

Every rule below is a corpus-derived rule: it names its anchor (`file:line` or census row id) and its sample size `n`, and it is judged by the named gate of `scripts/count_venue_gates.py`. **The bounded value itself — every floor, cap and band — lives in the instrument (the threshold registry, `--constants`) and is printed at run time by the gate that judges it.** A bound may still be *written* here, and several are, but only on a line that carries the licence its own `--constants` row declares: the citation (or census row id) that row names, or, on a line that carries no other citation, its own sample size `n`. `--restatement-check` is exactly that test — it flags a bound written **without** its licence, and `--restatement-report` prints every bound the prose does write together with the pointer that excused it, so the set is auditable rather than asserted. A criterion that needs a number to be readable as a criterion states the number's *shape* and the corpus row it came from. When you need the value without writing it, run the gate — from the skill-suite root (`cd skills-work/crypto-research-agent-skills`, the root every command in this file assumes) `python3 skills/crypto-venue-paper-writing/scripts/count_venue_gates.py --constants` prints every bound with its printing gate, its anchor and its `n`, and `--self-check` verifies the rules that keep it that way (the freeze rule, the two-way prose-versus-gate-name check, and the re-statement check). Write for the venue, not in the abstract: structural and stylistic decisions are made against one named conference's current official call, template, page limit, and reviewing culture.

## Scope

**Venue ownership — the binding boundary.** This skill owns the **crypto/LNCS venue family** (CRYPTO, EUROCRYPT, ASIACRYPT, and CRYPTO/LNCS-style submission) **plus** the corpus-calibrated compression, expansion, and budget rules that both venue families reuse.

**Delegated, and not restated here.** All **security-venue** register and rules belong to the two security skills — **ACM CCS**, **IEEE S&P** and **NDSS** to `ccs-writing-style`, **USENIX Security** to `usenixsec-writing-style`: their page budgets, the USENIX-mandated `Ethics considerations` and `Open science` sections together with their placement, the CCS IRB and responsible-disclosure obligations, and the per-venue overview and threat-model forms. This skill must not state a security-venue-specific rule; a draft aimed at those venues is written with the corresponding security skill, and this skill supplies only the shared corpus-calibrated rules. A rule that genuinely applies to both families is written here **once** and cited by the sibling skills — never duplicated into them. `description` lists every venue name because the frontmatter is a trigger list, not a claim of ownership.

In scope: repairing a draft that is wordy where it must be tight and thin where it must be deep; choosing a structure and section budget for the crypto/LNCS family; compressing an abstract and a contribution list to corpus-calibrated caps; enforcing a minimum technical-overview and construction substance and a named constituent checklist; measuring cross-section mass and relocating rather than deleting over-thick material; placing concrete numbers by section; naming and de-naming rules; applying an executable de-AI checklist; verifying double-blind anonymity; checking LNCS template formatting for the crypto family; and running a diagnosis-first pre-submission pass. Out of scope: inventing results, proofs, citations, or experiments; upgrading claim tiers; deciding novelty or authorship; guaranteeing acceptance; submitting without human authorization; and the delegated security-venue register and rules above, which this skill points to but does not own.

**Threshold provenance.** Every bounded value in this skill is derived from the frozen writing-craft corpora under `skills-work/corpus/` (the crypto/LNCS corpus and the security four-venue corpus) together with the EVH-MLPC revision record. Those `skills-work/…:file:line` citations are **provenance annotations into the authoring workspace's corpus snapshot, not links**, and they are not expected to resolve beside an installed copy. Every bound carries its sample size `n` and its measurement basis in the `--constants` registry row that `scripts/count_venue_gates.py` holds; no rule here is invented, and no bound is chosen by taste.

## Required Inputs

- the target venue and its **current** official call for papers (page limit, template, anonymity, ethics, artifact policy), fetched at execution time;
- the manuscript draft, or verified claims/evidence to write from, in LaTeX **and** its markdown twin when both exist (the rules measure either, but never mix them in one comparison);
- a named region map: which `file:line` span is the abstract, the contribution list, the technical overview, the construction, the security analysis, and each appendix;
- the comparison corpus for the related-work and comparison-table positioning;
- the venue template or bibliography style, when a format pass is requested.

**The region map is an input, and two gates exist to say when one is missing from it.** Gate **`abstract`** and gate **`contributions`** are **region-presence rows**, not thresholds: each is emitted once, and only in the branch where the region map supplies no span for its region — the `abstract` row when the map has no `abstract` key, the `contributions` row when it has no `contributions` key. Each reports status `unmatched` with the reason "no … region given", carries **no bound, no floor and no cap**, and is not a measurement of the draft. They exist because a required region that cannot be located is reported rather than silently absent: the instrument's own doctrine is that an unlocatable required region **is itself a breach of the rule that it must be locatable**, so an `unmatched` here is a finding and a non-zero exit, never a pass. Everything the two regions are actually judged on belongs to criteria 1–9 and to the gates named there (`abstract-words`, `abstract-build-share`, `abstract-longest-sentence`, `abstract-paragraphs`, `contributions-block`, `contributions-items`, `abstract-contributions-duplication`), which are `measured` whenever the region is supplied.

## Success Criteria

Every criterion names the corpus evidence it rests on — an anchor (`file:line` or census row id) and a sample size `n` — and the gate of `scripts/count_venue_gates.py` that judges it. Every bounded value is printed by that gate; run `--constants` for the registry. A criterion stated as an adjective, or one whose evidence has no anchor, does not satisfy it.

**Condensation — the abstract** (`references/compression-rules.md`):

1. Abstract body is **at most the corpus median abstract length** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:121`, n=59 tier-A; gate **`abstract-words`** prints the cap and the draft's count). A longer abstract is not automatically wrong, but the length must be licensed by that corpus line; a longer cap stated on its own does **not** satisfy this rule.
2. **At least the corpus's pooled build share** of abstract words describe what is built (build share, `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:165`, n=15 hand-coded four-move sample; gate **`abstract-build-share`**). This is the criterion that catches an abstract spending two thirds of itself on proved bounds and giving its construction one sentence: such a draft can pass any length cap.
3. Longest abstract sentence is **at most the corpus median of per-abstract longest sentences** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:124`, n=59, whose third quartile is on the same line; gate **`abstract-longest-sentence`**).
4. `## Workflow` prints the abstract's total word count, its per-paragraph move labelling, its build share, and its longest sentence, before and after the rewrite.

**Condensation — the contribution list** (`references/compression-rules.md`; gates **`contributions-block`** and **`contributions-items`**):

5. **The corpus's own bullet range** (corpus median 4 bullets, `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:196`, n=50 tier-A; endpoints printed by gate **`contributions-block`**).
6. **At most the corpus median words per bullet** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:198`, n=214 bullets) and the longest bullet **at most its own corpus median** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:199`, n=50; gate **`contributions-items`**). Both are the corpus median used unrounded, so each cap is exactly the number its cited line carries. **The cap is a cryptography-family value and does not govern a security-venue contribution list, and the security figure does not govern a crypto one:** each side is measured on its own corpus and cited on its own line — crypto at `:198` (n=214), security at `skills-work/corpus/security/statistics.md:59` (n=22, printed by `ccs-writing-style`'s gate) — and they differ **by construction, not by disagreement**, because a crypto bullet is a one-line result statement while a security contribution item is a paragraph-length claim carrying its evidence. Recorded as the venue side of the t8 review's cross-family finding (R-VENUE-37); each family's value is read from its own instrument's registry row — the crypto side from `--constants` here, the security side from `ccs-writing-style`'s own instrument, which is what prints it — never copied.
7. Whole block is **at most the corpus median block length** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:197`, n=50) and **at most the corpus median block/abstract ratio** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:200`, n=50; both printed by gate **`contributions-block`**). A list longer than the abstract it summarises is the signature of an expanded abstract rather than a compressed list.
8. **Zero shared 6-grams** between the abstract and the contribution block, on the corpus metric: 6-grams over case-folded `[a-z0-9]+` tokens, counted absolutely and as a ratio over the abstract's 6-grams (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:206-212`, n=50; gate **`abstract-contributions-duplication`** prints count, cap and ratio). Where a shared 6-gram is a named object introduced in both places, record the disposition instead of deleting the name.
9. Every span that leaves the abstract or the list **has a destination** — a named section, a footnote, or an appendix — and the destination is written down. Nothing is deleted outright.

**Elaboration — the technical overview** (`references/expansion-rules.md`; gate **`overview`**):

10. A separately titled overview section is **not** mandatory: the share of tier-A papers that have one is a minority (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:229`, n=62), so a title-keyed requirement misses the majority. The overview's **content and mass** are mandatory instead.
11. The overview is **at least the corpus minimum overview length** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:239`, n=18); the corpus's **first quartile** on the same row is the practical target for a construction-centred paper.
12. The overview is **at least the corpus minimum in source lines** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:240`, n=18) and **at least the corpus first quartile of the introduction's words** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:241`, n=18). Where the overview lives as a named block inside the introduction instead, its corpus minimum is on `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:252` (n=5).
13. The overview carries the five mandatory constituents listed in `references/expansion-rules.md`, each anchored to a corpus line, and **at least the corpus first quartile in named sub-blocks** (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:243`). The sub-block count carries no verdict in the instrument; the mandatory-constituent list is what judges this criterion.
    **The overview's own formal floor — binding, and it is a floor, not a diagnostic (t1's F-6).** Inline mathematics per 1000 words over the **declared overview span**, judged against the corpus's own **overview-role** row: gate **`overview-formal-density`** prints the floor of **11.70** inline-math pairs per 1000 words (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:650`, overview role, n=19, the corpus **minimum**), and prints that row's first quartile and median as **context only**. **What makes it a floor is the row's zero-count:** no measured overview in that population is formula-free, so a formula-free overview measures 0 and the gate returns a breach — the same defect criterion 20 catches over the whole manuscript, caught here on the span that is supposed to carry the mechanism. Declaring no overview span is reported `unmatched` **with** a breach, because 0 must never read as a pass. The floor is over the span, so it travels with the overview wherever the overview lives (criterion 10).

**Elaboration — the construction** (`references/expansion-rules.md`):

14. Construction-to-analysis mass ratio on **words, `direct` basis** (a section's own text with its children's spans subtracted; gate **`construction-analysis-ratio`**): the corpus's **first quartile is the fail boundary**, the interval up to its **median needs expansion**, at or above the median it passes (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:285`, n=10). **The gate prints the ratio it measured and the band that ratio fell in — not the two boundaries** (measured: `36/36 = 1.0000 on the direct basis -> pass (at or above median)`, with neither `0.44` nor `0.89` in its output). The boundaries are the registry rows `RATIO_FAIL_BELOW` and `RATIO_PASS_AT` in `--constants`.
    **Degeneracy guard — mandatory, and registered in the R-VENUE-13 row rather than only here (R-15).** Before printing any band verdict, test the basis: a section's `direct` measurement is **degenerate** when its `direct` word count is smaller than the word count of its own heading, or smaller than the instrument's minimum direct-word count, or its `direct` span holds no running prose (only heading lines and fragments). **When the guard fires the rule must not print the band verdicts and must not cite the ratio bands:** it reports only the hierarchical numbers, names the twin (`tex` or `md`) and the unit (raw lines, content lines, or words), and names the sections that tripped the guard. It decides real verdicts, not edge cases: on the calibration manuscript §3/§4/§5 carry no direct prose, so their `direct` spans collapse onto heading lines and the unguarded ratio would call `pass` for a §3 that criterion 15's checklist calls thin.
15. The construction section carries the constituent checklist in `references/expansion-rules.md`, with **at least five items**, each anchored to a corpus line. This checklist, not the ratio, is the discriminating instrument, and it is the fallback whenever the guard above fires; its discriminating power is in the corpus's quartile table (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:351-370`). A calibration caution that must travel with any verdict from it — the frozen manuscript's §3 is thin for its **parameter list** and **roadmap** reasons, and its §4 passes the checklist in full — lives in `references/expansion-rules.md` §The corrected §3 and §4 verdicts, which is the file that owns the manuscript-specific readings (R-5: a rule line does not carry a manuscript literal). Two corpus warnings: the automated counts are a **lower bound** (authors rename heading macros, so a named block may exist undetected), and two constituents are genuinely absent from the corpus (`parameter_choice_reason` at `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:311` and `worked_example` at `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:319`), so their absence is **not** a defect — do not add them to tick the list, and do not promote another minority device (criterion 16) to compensate. Two qualifications on criterion 14: the `direct` basis is defined in the corpus's basis section (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:18-19`), and a rule of the form "the construction exceeds the analysis" is not supported — it holds for a minority of corpus pairs (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:288`) and a second, differently-based measurement exists (median, first quartile, appendices included, n=17; `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:290`) — so a rule must name its basis and may not mix bases.
16. **The construction floor is the corpus's own DISJUNCTION, and its joint pass rate is disclosed (R-30).** At least **one** of `formal_object_box`, `labelled_parameter_list`, `correctness_claim` must be present in the construction section. The corpus states the rule that way itself — "a construction section that contains **none** of the box / parameter-list / correctness group is below what this corpus does" (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:321-323`). Gate **`construction-floor`** prints the disjunction's measured joint pass rate with its section universe (published universe `skills-work/reports/formal-content-audit.md` §2d, n=36; the gate also prints this file's own reproduction and names both populations rather than merging them, R-30.6), the joint rate of the **repealed** five- and six-item conjunction, and the per-paper satisfied-count distribution of the reproduction.
    **The finding to carry (R-30.1):** an all-items-required conjunction of five or six constituents rejects almost the entire corpus, so it is **repealed** — a floor almost the whole corpus fails is a fiction, and it would have told the author to add a `reader_roadmap` to every construction section while only a minority of the corpus has one. Adding `formal_object_box` to the conjunction **rejects no additional paper** (both counts are printed), so its marginal discriminating power *inside a conjunction* is zero: a second, independent reason the binding form is the disjunction.
    **Every other constituent is a diagnostic** with its incidence — the gate carries the whole row, and `correctness_claim`'s baseline (`corpus/crypto/statistics.md:307`, n=29), `formal_object_box`'s share of both quartiles (`:358`) and `complexity_statement`'s (`:355`) are on those lines — and carries no verdict (R-30.3). `formal_object_box` is in the disjunction as a **presence** requirement only: this file does **not** claim it discriminates thin from thick. `complexity_statement` is deliberately **not** in the disjunction — a device that separates nothing may not be required.
    **The 0-formula defect is closed by TWO instruments, not one (R-30.4):** the whole-manuscript `formal-density` floor of criterion 20 and this disjunction. The density floor catches a paper with no formula anywhere; the disjunction catches a construction section that carries none of the three named constituents even if the paper has formulas elsewhere.
17. **Display equations — a presence floor, judged on ONE declared basis (equation-family environments), licensed by the zero-rate rule.** A manuscript must carry **at least one** equation-family environment (`equation`, `align`, `alignat`, `gather`, `multline`, `flalign`, `eqnarray`, `subequations`, `IEEEeqnarray`, `dmath`, `displaymath`, `math`, `empheq`). **The judging basis is the equation family**, the basis of the row that licenses the rule (`corpus/crypto/statistics.md:629`, whose numerator, denominator and zero-share gate **`display-density`** prints). The corpus quantiles on `:618` are **context and never the floor**: a quantile floor would fail accepted papers.
    **Bracketed displays (`\[…\]`) are a DIFFERENT basis, reported beside the verdict and never merged into it (R-33.2).** The combined count ("bracketed + equation-family") may carry a zero-rule **only when the caller names a census row measured on that same numerator**, via `--combined-display-row <ROW-ID>`, and the row must pass a **row-basis referee** that reads the row's own heading at run time. Two rows exist and are **not interchangeable**: the inline-inclusive combined row (`skills-work/audit/evidence-structure-census.md`, row `A19`) is **refused** here because this gate's combined count **excludes** inline, while the display-only combined row (`A20`, **excludes inline**) is the row this gate's count may be judged on. A row that resolves but whose heading does not say whether inline is included or excluded is **refused too** — an unreadable basis is not a licence, and a refused row exits non-zero as a measurement failure instead of being substituted. R-33.3: widening a basis to make a gate pass and narrowing one to make it fail are **the same defect**.
    **Two measured cautions travel with row `A20` and neither may be dropped (census, row `A20`).** (a) The frozen calibration manuscript sits **below** A20's first quartile while clearing census row `A1` comfortably, so **binding A20's quartile as a floor would fail a manuscript whose total mathematical load is corpus-normal** — the same failure family as a single-axis float floor, and the reason the binding floor on this axis stays the equation-family **presence** rule above. (b) The axis separates **formatting, not mathematical content**: the papers below A20's first quartile carry inline-only densities at the corpus's own inline median, as the row records. A20 is therefore **evidence**, and the gate's judgement on it is the zero-rule **only when a caller explicitly names it**.
    **Basis substitution is failure mode F12** and is this run's dominant family. **The rule names the twin it judges (R-28.3):** the frozen manuscript's two twins disagree on this axis — its LaTeX source carries bracketed displays and no equation-family environment, its markdown twin `$$` blocks — so "display = 0" is meaningful only with its basis written beside it, and a disagreement is a **finding**, never a tie-break.
18. **Notation block — exactly one declared named span (t1's F-5).** The symbol set is defined in **one** named span, declared in the region map. Gate **`notation-block`** prints the tier-A presence share of a notation block and the baseline share of a `symbol_notation` constituent *inside a construction section* (1 of 29, `corpus/crypto/statistics.md:317`); the second travels with the rule so it is **never** read as requiring the notation block to sit inside the construction. A pass that declares no such span reports `unmatched` **with** a breach: a measured zero must never read as a pass.
19. Section role is decided by **what a section presents**, never by its title: most tier-A papers have no section whose title contains "Construction" (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:112`), so `Description ...` and `Parameters.` blocks count as construction material.

**The formal apparatus** (`references/expansion-rules.md` §The formal-content floors; anchors in `skills-work/audit/evidence-structure-census.md`, whose rows `A1`–`A20` are append-only, alongside its security rows `B1`–`B6` and its gap rows `G-E1`–`G-E9`):

20. **Formal density — whole manuscript, binding.** Inline mathematics (a `$…$` pair) per 1000 alnum tokens over the **whole manuscript**: **at least the tier-A first quartile** (`skills-work/audit/evidence-structure-census.md` row **A1**; n=62; basis `[A-Za-z0-9]+` over the comment-stripped source; gate **`formal-density`** prints the floor, the corpus median and maximum, and the population's zero-count). A draft at zero is not "low on a distribution" — it is outside it. The floor is the first quartile and **not** the median: a median floor would fail half the corpus *and* the frozen calibration manuscript, whose own measured density the gate prints beside the floor. This is the **only binding floor on this axis at this scope**; the all-127-papers first quartile on the same row and the corpus median are **context only**, and the gate labels them as such. **Scope is part of the basis (R-27):** the row is a **whole-paper** row, so the binding form is whole-paper. **The region-scoped measurement is not a diagnostic either — it has its own BINDING floor on a different basis (criterion 20a, R-32 F-4)**, judged on the corpus's per-role table rather than on row `A1`; the two figures print side by side and neither may be substituted for the other (R-33.3). What carries no verdict is the narrower case: a span whose role the caller cannot name, which has no corpus row and is reported `unmatched` rather than compared with a whole-paper quantile. "The construction section contains no mathematics at all" is also caught **structurally**, by criterion 16.
20a. **The formal regions have their own BINDING floor, on §FORMAL.2's basis (R-32 F-4).** The construction/analysis span must carry at least the **corpus first quartile** of inline-math spans per 1000 words for a **construction** role (n=36) or the corresponding **analysis**-role first quartile (n=40), from the corpus's per-role table (`corpus/crypto/statistics.md` §FORMAL.2, reproducible in the authoring workspace with `python3 skills-work/corpus/crypto/tools/formal_by_role.py`, **run from the workspace root — the directory that contains `skills-work/`**; that path is a workspace instrument and does not resolve beside an installed copy, per §Threshold provenance); gate **`formal-density-role`** prints both floors, both medians, both zero-counts and the caller's role. **The caller declares the role** (`--formal-role`): the floor is role-scoped, nothing may infer which row applies, and an undeclared role is reported `unmatched`, never guessed. **This replaces an earlier claim that was untrue (FALSE, and its reason for keeping this axis non-binding is withdrawn):** a previous revision of this skill and of R-27 claimed "no region-scoped corpus row exists"; that claim is FALSE and was used to keep the axis non-binding. The corpus carries role-scoped rows with their own basis, so the claim is deleted and the floor is **binding**. The measured consequence is printed by the gate: the calibration manuscript's §4 sits **below the construction first quartile** — the section carrying this paper's contribution sits below corpus practice, exactly the defect the user reported ("具体方案写得太少"), reported and not suppressed. **Two bases, never merged (R-33.3):** this floor is on §FORMAL.2's basis (floats and `$$` displays removed for the numerator, whitespace words over the analyzer's rendering for the denominator) while criterion 20's is on census row `A1`'s alnum-source-token basis (floats included); the two print side by side and **neither substitutes for the other**.
21. **Citation-key density — whole manuscript, binding.** Citation keys per 1000 alnum tokens over the whole manuscript: **at least the tier-A first quartile** (`census A12`, n=62; gate **`citation-density`**; the row's median and per-paper key count are **context only** and the gate labels them). On a markdown twin the keys are counted with the bracket convention `[12, 13]`.
22. **Reference calls — whole manuscript, binding; resolvability — whole manuscript, binding.** `\ref`/`\eqref`/`\autoref`/`\cref` per 1000 alnum tokens: **at least census row `A10`'s own per-1000-word first quartile** (census row `A10`, n=62 tier-A; gate **`ref-density`** prints it with the row's median and the all-papers recomputation). A density rule needs a density row: almost every tier-A paper making at least one reference call is **presence, not the threshold** (setting the floor at one call per 1000 words would quote a value no census row publishes, an order of magnitude below the corpus). And **every reference target must have a `\label` in the same twin**, with **at least one label**: no tier-A paper has zero labels (`census` row `A11`; its minimum, median and labels-to-references ratio are **context**, printed by gate **`ref-resolvability`**). An unresolved target is a broken pointer: a **measurement failure**, reported with the unmatched set named, never a style note.
23. **Statement and proof apparatus — the floor is on their SUM (census row `A18`), with the two single axes as context and a `not-applicable` filing for non-theorem genres.** Report the whole-paper counts of statement environments (theorem, lemma, corollary, proposition, claim, fact, definition, assumption, remark, example, observation, conjecture, invariant, construction) and of `proof` environments, and judge their **combined** per-1000-word density against **at least the combined row's first quartile** (census row `A18`, tier-A n=62; gate **`statement-apparatus`** prints the row's median, third quartile and per-paper zero-rate — the last of which is what licenses a floor here). **Why the floor is on the sum and not on either axis.** A floor on `theorem_envs` alone (census row `A3`) or on `proof_envs` alone (census row `A4`) would be a **genre** judgement: the census records papers with a theorem environment and no proof, and papers with the reverse, and this skill's own recalculation of the combined row over the tier-A papers reproduces the published first quartile. So `A3`/`A4` print as **context only** and the **sum** carries the bound. A paper with no statement apparatus at all breaches; a paper with a theorem but no proof does not. **The filing still exists for the non-theorem genre:** a draft with **zero** combined apparatus is judged leniently **only** if a `not-applicable` filing is recorded, and the filing does **not** discharge the density floor — a sparse-but-nonzero apparatus is still below corpus practice. **Population caveat.** The floor is on the **tier-A** population; over **all 127** measured papers the same quantity collapses to a first quartile at zero with many papers below it, so a draft outside tier-A practice would breach, and the gate prints that caveat beside the number.
24. **Float forms — a floor on the COMBINED row, crypto/LNCS regime.** Float forms (`figure` environment + `table` float + `tabular` body) per 1000 alnum tokens over the whole manuscript: **at least the tier-A first quartile of the combined row** (`census` row **A17**; n=62; the row's per-paper and per-1000-word medians print as **context only**), **and** a draft with **no** figure, **no** table float and **no** `tabular` body breaches by the **zero-rate rule** — the row's per-paper zero-rate is below the licence threshold at which a floor becomes meaningless, and gate **`float-presence`** prints both. **Why the floor is on the combined row and not on the single-form rows:** `figure` (row A6), the `table` float (row A7) and `algorithm`/`protocol` boxes (row A9) all carry zero-rates that would misjudge a quarter to nearly half of the accepted corpus, so a floor on any of them would be a genre judgement; the gate prints all three as **context** and they never judge. **No figure-only or algorithm-box-only verdict is emitted:** the corpus's own worked case shows a figure-keyed gate fires on a float-rich manuscript (the frozen calibration manuscript has no figure but many float forms, and gate **`figure-and-box-tail`** measures the figure-and-box combination separately from A17) while missing the sample draft's real deficits, which lie on the rows above. The security-venue regimes on this dimension are **not** restated here: they belong to the two security skills. **Twin note.** On a markdown twin the `tabular` unit is counted through the **pipe-table proxy** (`(?:^\|.*\n){2,}`), the same proxy the census instrument uses; the census warns the two are comparable in *kind* but not in *unit*, so a markdown count is always printed with the proxy named and never averaged with a LaTeX count.
25. **Source composition is reported, never judged.** The share of source lines inside a float or a math environment is printed as a **diagnostic with no floor and no ceiling** (census rows `A14`/`A15`, n=62; their first quartiles print as context by gate **`source-composition`**), so that a draft at zero on both rows is visibly an outlier on two rows at once.

**Budget and placement** (`references/section-budget.md`, `references/numeric-placement.md`):

26. No single subsection holds a **majority** of its parent section's words: above the corpus's **majority share** a relocation pass is required, and above its **outlier share** the parent is an outlier. Gate **`largest-child-ceiling`** prints every parent-child share it judged and the band that share fell in; the two boundaries themselves are the registry rows `CEILING_MAJORITY` and `CEILING_OUTLIER` in `--constants` (the outlier boundary's line is `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:334`, and the share of pairs above the majority boundary is at `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:333`). The corpus median largest-child share, its third quartile, its 90th percentile and both tail shares are the Q4.3 distribution over 561 parent-child pairs — 561 is the sample size on its population line, `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:326` — whose values sit at `skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:330`, `:333` and `:334`. 
27. Relocation is **move-not-delete**: every claim, status label, number, and provenance travels with the moved text. Verification records, review narrative, superseded metrics, measurement tables, and tool output are relocatable; claims and their status wording are not.
28. Concrete measured values appear in evaluation, parameters, and implementation material, and **never** in construction or proof sections — the corpus audit finds exactly zero high-precision literals in those roles (`skills-work/corpus/crypto/statistics.md:405-415`). A concrete value may sit in a theory section only when it carries a filed reason of the class *analytic or design-point arithmetic, not a measurement*; measurement records belong in a heading-marked evidence record.
29. Process-register naming never appears as an example or a prescription, and the de-naming pass follows the **adjudication contract owned by `references/de-ai-and-style.md`** — that file is the authority for the classes, the definition test, and the permitted-literal blocks, and this criterion must not restate it. The pass is satisfied when **every class-1/3/6 candidate has been adjudicated**, each with either its **definition anchor** or `no definition found`, and there are **zero unresolvable identifiers**. Raw per-class hit counts are **diagnostics only and must never be presented as the verdict**. A literal may appear only inside the five blocks that must name one in order to be usable — the anti-pattern table, §Stage 2's definition-test row, the class-6 discussion, the worked adjudication, and the reproducible command — and each anti-pattern entry must carry its forbidding sentence.
30. **Status contract for the two mass rules — this is what makes the guard falsifiable (R-15).** The section-mass pass is executed by the **shipped instrument**, which measures each named region on a named twin and reports, for every section it evaluates, exactly one status: `measured` (a number was produced), `degenerate` (the `direct` guard of criterion 14 fired for that section), or `unmatched` (a named section could not be located in the twin). **`degenerate` and `unmatched` are the required statuses when the guard fires or a section is not found — never a silent pass and never an omitted row.** A pass that prints a band verdict in either case, or that omits the section from the report, has failed. The same statuses apply to the ceiling of criterion 26, with one structural exception recorded in `references/section-budget.md`: the ceiling compares a child against its parent on **hierarchical** counts and never subtracts direct spans, so it cannot be `degenerate` — only `measured` or `unmatched`. The instrument's **non-zero exit codes** separate a broken measurement, a breached threshold, and a failed verdict path, so a zero-formula draft is a **non-zero-exit event**, not a clean run with a note; the codes and their flags are the instrument's own contract (module docstring and `--help`), and this criterion fixes what the *statuses* mean rather than restating the mechanics.
    **Binding scope (R-27).** The compression, overview, ratio and budget gates are region-scoped and binding. The formal-apparatus gates of criteria 20–25 are **whole-manuscript** gates, because the rows that license their values are whole-paper rows. **The region-scoped formal rows are binding too, on their own role-scoped rows (R-32 F-4).** Gate **`formal-density-role`** judges the caller's construction/analysis span against the corpus's **construction**-role or **analysis**-role first quartile (`corpus/crypto/statistics.md` §FORMAL.2); the caller declares which with `--formal-role`, an undeclared role is reported `unmatched` rather than guessed, and the gate prints a breach verdict when the span falls under the role's floor — on the frozen calibration manuscript it prints exactly that verdict for the construction role. Gate **`overview-formal-density`** judges the declared overview span against the corpus's overview-role minimum, and it prints a breach verdict rather than a note. **Neither is a "non-binding diagnostic", and no sentence in this file or its references may call them one.** The claim that "no region-scoped corpus row exists", which an earlier revision of this skill and of R-27 used to keep this axis non-binding, is **FALSE and is withdrawn at criterion 20a and here**. What genuinely carries no verdict is narrower than the earlier text implied: an **ad-hoc span whose role the caller cannot name** has no corpus row to be judged against, so the instrument reports it `unmatched` instead of substituting a whole-paper quantile for it.
    **Corpus thresholds are cited by ANCHOR ID, never by a line number (R-29),** because the census is rewritten in place as rows are appended. The instrument holds **no hard-coded census line number**: `--validate-anchors` resolves every anchor it cites from **the row's own heading at run time**, prints the line each one currently resolves to, and exits non-zero if any does not — a **stale citation is a measurement failure**, not a silent drift — and it prints the sha256 of the census revision it verified against (R-31.5). The **region map is an input with no default** (`--regions`, or `--calibration-regions` after a sha256 check against the pinned revision); a missing map, an out-of-range span, or a title-based construction/analysis guess is refused rather than filled in.
    **Three rules keep this file honest (t2).** The **freeze rule** is a closed gate inventory: a gate may not be added without simultaneously merging with or deleting an existing one, and `--check-freeze` compares the inventory against the names the source's own gate calls can emit, failing in both directions. The **two-way prose-versus-gate-name rule** runs `--gate-names`: direction A fails on a backticked gate-like token no `gate(...)` call can emit, and direction B fails on a frozen gate named nowhere in this skill's prose. The **re-statement rule** is a licence test, not a ban on numbers: a bound written in this file or `references/` is tolerated exactly when its line carries the licence its own `--constants` row declares — the citation or census row that row names, or its own `n` where the line carries no other citation — and `--restatement-check` flags the bounds that are written **without** one, so the true statement is "every bound here carries its licence", never "this file recites no bound". `--restatement-report` prints each written bound with the pointer that excused it, and `--constants` prints the registry with every printing gate and anchor. All three run in `--self-check`.

## Failure Modes

- **Words where it must be tight** (gates **`abstract-words`**, **`contributions-block`**, **`contributions-items`**, **`abstract-contributions-duplication`**, each printing the cap it used). An abstract over its cap, a contribution list over its cap or past its bullet range, a bullet over its per-item cap, or a shared 6-gram between abstract and list: run the abstract compression pass in `references/compression-rules.md` and return the four counts plus the destination of every removed span. A near-miss — an abstract inside its length cap but with a build share far below the floor — still fails, and is reported as an allocation defect rather than a length defect.
- **Thin where it must be deep** (gate **`overview`**). An overview under its floor, under its share of the introduction, or missing a mandatory constituent: run the expansion pass and name every absent constituent. An overview that "reads complete" while sitting under the floor is the reported defect, not a judgement call.
- **Prose where formal content must be.** A construction section carrying none of the three constituents of criterion 16's disjunction; an inline-math density below the floor of criterion 20; no **display** equation at all (criterion 17); no declared notation span (criterion 18); a citation-key density below criterion 21's floor; a reference-call density below criterion 22's floor; a reference target with no `\label`; no float form at all; or a statement/proof apparatus of zero with no `not-applicable` filing. A prose-only gate set cannot see these: **a draft with zero formulas, zero cross-references and zero labels passed every gate of the previously shipped set**, so each axis now carries a binding gate of its own. **These are two independent instruments, not one** (R-30.4): a per-section **existence** requirement (criterion 16's disjunction — one section may satisfy it with a single labelled object) and whole-manuscript **density** floors (criteria 17–24 — which that same draft can fail in every region). Report which instrument produced each finding, and report every finding's measured **number**, its **scope** (whole manuscript or named span) and its corpus **anchor**: the instrument prints the floor value itself, so a failure entry here does not restate it.
- **Length-only compliance.** A draft that satisfies the caps but inverts the allocation, or expands everywhere rather than relocating, has not passed. Report the allocation and the ratio bands, not just the totals.
- **Unnamed basis.** Any printed ratio, page count, or word budget that does not state which twin (`tex` or `md`), which unit (raw lines, content lines, or words), and which basis (`direct` or hierarchical) is unusable; remeasure before reporting.
- **Title-keyed structure.** A section classified as "construction" or "analysis" from its heading alone mis-files the majority of papers. Classify by content and list the sections you classified.
- **Numbers parked in a theory section.** A measured value in a construction or proof section without a filed analytic/design-point reason is the re-induced form of the reported defect: relocate it with its digits and provenance intact.
- **Fabrication.** No result, citation, parameter, measurement, or review outcome may be invented. A threshold that cannot be traced to a corpus `file:line` is not a threshold; write the rule without a number instead of choosing one.
- **AI-flavor prose that survives a first pass.** Run the grep-level checklist in `references/de-ai-and-style.md` and rewrite; do not merely flag.
- **Venue rules may have changed.** Never assert a remembered page limit or style file: fetch the current official call and record a dated snapshot.
- **De-identification gap** (author names, institution, identifying repository links) must be fixed before any format pass.

## Workflow

Diagnose before rewriting. Steps 1-2 produce the diagnosis; a rewrite that starts before them cannot say which section is thin and which is redundant.

1. **Freeze the venue and the region map.** Fetch the current official call and template; record the date. Produce the `file:line` map from `## Required Inputs` for both twins when both exist. Do not proceed to any budget claim without it.
2. **Run the diagnosis pass.** For each region, print: abstract words, sentences, per-paragraph move labelling and build share; contribution block words, bullet count, longest bullet, and abstract-to-list shared 6-grams; each section's raw lines, content lines, and words, with the largest-child share per parent; the construction-to-analysis ratio with its basis; the overview's words, lines, and share of the introduction; and the **formal apparatus** — inline-math density over the whole manuscript (criterion 20) **and** over the declared construction/analysis span (**also binding**, criterion 20a, on the corpus's per-role row), the overview span's own binding formal floor (`overview-formal-density`), citation-key density, reference-call density, the label/resolvability set, the statement/proof counts with the filing or its absence, float presence, and the source-composition diagnostic. Output a ranked list that names the under-detailed section and the redundant section with their numbers. Generic advice is not an acceptable output of this step.
3. **Run the abstract compression pass** (`references/compression-rules.md`). Cut in this order: process narration, meta-narration, abstract-to-list duplication, adjectives standing where a number belongs. Move, never delete: write each removed span's destination. Re-report the four counts.
4. **Run the expansion pass** (`references/expansion-rules.md`). Bring each region up to its floor and its mandatory constituents. Every added sentence must restate material already in the manuscript, in the design record, or in an artifact — never a new claim.
5. **Run the section-mass and relocation pass** (`references/section-budget.md`). Relocate over-thick ledger material to a heading-marked evidence record, carrying every claim, status label, and number with it.
6. **Run the numeric-placement pass** (`references/numeric-placement.md`). Scan the construction and proof regions for the concrete-literal class; relocate each hit with its digits and provenance, or file the analytic/design-point reason that licenses it.
7. **Run the de-identification and de-naming pass** (`references/de-ai-and-style.md`, which owns the classes, the definition test, and the permitted-literal blocks). Collect the class-1/3/6 candidates, then **adjudicate every one of them against the manuscript and write the verdict**: `violation` with `no definition found`, or `legitimate(a)`/`(b)`/`(c)` with its definition anchor, or `not-a-token` for a match that is not a token at all. The pass is **not** "zero hits" — it is **no unadjudicated hits and zero unresolvable identifiers**, and the raw per-class counts are diagnostics that must never be presented as the verdict.
8. **Run the pre-submission pass.** Number-and-body consistency, claim tiers, citation integrity, double-blind scan, page budget against the fetched call, template compile. Report every unresolved item as a question for the human author rather than resolving it silently.

## Output Contract

Return, in this order, and keep the diagnosis visible even when the rewrite is complete:

1. **Target-venue policy snapshot** with the fetch date and the source URL.
2. **Diagnosis table** — one row per region, with the measured numbers, the threshold it was compared against, and a verdict of `redundant`, `thin`, `over-thick`, or `within budget`. A row with no number is not a diagnosis.
3. **Rewrite or diffs**, with every removed span's destination recorded.
4. **The four abstract numbers** before and after: total words, build share, longest sentence, paragraph move labelling.
5. **The five contribution-list numbers** before and after: bullets, block words, longest bullet, block/abstract ratio, shared 6-grams.
6. **Expansion report** — overview words/lines/share with the absent constituents named; construction ratio with its named basis and the constituent checklist with its present/absent marks, including the required formal-object constituent of criterion 16.
6a. **Formal-apparatus report** — one row per formal gate, each with its measured value, its **scope** (whole manuscript for the whole-paper rows; the named span for the role-scoped and overview rows, which are binding on their own corpus rows under R-32 F-4), its threshold or, where the row genuinely carries no bound, its explicit **non-binding** label, and its corpus anchor: inline-math density (whole manuscript under criterion 20, and the declared construction/analysis span under criterion 20a), the overview span's own formal floor (`overview-formal-density`), citation-key density, reference-call density, label/resolvability set, statement/proof counts with the filing (or its absence), float presence, and the source-composition diagnostic. A row without a scope is not a report, and **an `unmatched` row is reported as a finding rather than omitted**.
7. **Budget report** — per-section and per-subsection shares with the largest-child verdict, and the relocation ledger.
8. **Numeric-placement report** and the **de-naming and de-AI checklist results**.
9. **Venue compliance report** — double-blind, page limit, template, bibliography style, against the fetched call.
10. **Unresolved items** requiring the human author, each with the reason it could not be decided here.
