# Top-Venue Readiness Gates

These are rigor targets, not acceptance guarantees. Refresh the intended venue's official rules before use; deadlines, formats, anonymity rules, artifact processes, ethics requirements, and disclosure policies change.

## Gates

1. **Venue fit and significance** — the problem matters to the venue audience and the contribution is stated precisely.
2. **Literature and novelty** — search boundaries are reproducible; closest work is compared claim by claim; residual novelty risk is explicit.
3. **Correctness and security** — definitions, assumptions, theorem statements, reductions, and protocol properties are separated and audited.
4. **Parameters and complexity** — asymptotic, symbolic, estimated, and measured costs are not conflated; concrete parameters are reproducible.
5. **Evaluation and artifacts** — baselines are fair, negative results are retained, environments are frozen, and each empirical claim maps to an artifact or documented limitation.
6. **Ethics and responsible disclosure** — human researchers resolve privacy, dual-use, vulnerability, consent, data, and deployment risks.
7. **Authorship and disclosure** — accountable human authors approve authorship, conflicts, funding, acknowledgments, and required AI/tool disclosures.
8. **Presentation and compliance** — the current official venue instructions govern format, anonymity, supplemental material, and submission mechanics.
9. **Adversarial pre-review** — blocking novelty, correctness, security, reproducibility, ethics, and presentation issues are resolved and re-checked.
