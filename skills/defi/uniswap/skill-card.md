## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Users may treat Uniswap usage guidance as financial advice or as authority to execute transactions automatically.

Mitigation: Use the skill as educational guidance only; independently verify transaction details and do not allow automatic transaction execution based on the skill output.

Risk: Token spoofing, unsafe approvals, MEV exposure, slippage, and liquidity-position losses can cause financial loss.

Mitigation: Verify token contract addresses with official sources, review slippage and price impact, prefer MEV protection where appropriate, and limit or revoke token approvals.
