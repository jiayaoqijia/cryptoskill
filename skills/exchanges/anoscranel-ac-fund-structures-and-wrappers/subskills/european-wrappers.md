# European wrappers

## Standard

The UCITS framework sets minimum standards for a retail fund in the European Union — eligible assets, diversification limits, leverage restrictions, and disclosure — and a fund that meets them can be sold across member states under a passport. That passport is the wrapper's commercial purpose: a single authorisation opens a distribution channel across many jurisdictions.

The corporate form is separate from the passport. A SICAV is an investment company with variable capital, an OEIC is the United Kingdom equivalent, and a unit trust is a trust structure; all three can carry a UCITS label, and the label describes the investment rules rather than the legal form. Reading a European fund means recording both: the form determines the investor's legal relationship with the vehicle, and the label determines the constraints the manager operates under.

Primary sources: <https://www.esma.europa.eu/> and <https://www.fca.org.uk/>.

## Decision rules

- Record the legal form: SICAV, OEIC, unit trust, or another vehicle.
- Record whether the fund is UCITS-compliant and what limits that imposes.
- Record the domicile and the regulator that authorised it.
- Record the passport status and the jurisdictions in which it is registered for sale.
- Record the eligible-asset and diversification limits the wrapper enforces.
- Record the redemption terms, including any notice period and any permitted suspension.

## Procedure

1. Read the prospectus for the legal form and domicile.
2. Record the UCITS status and the limits it imposes.
3. Record the authorising regulator.
4. Record the registered-for-sale jurisdictions.
5. Record redemption terms and suspension powers.
6. Write the wrapper summary with the form and the label distinguished.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating UCITS as a legal form rather than a rule set.
- Assuming a passport covers every jurisdiction.
- Ignoring the redemption notice period.
- Overlooking the suspension power, which can trap holders in stress.
- Comparing a UCITS with an unregulated vehicle as if the labels were equivalent.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Legal form recorded.
- [ ] UCITS status and limits recorded.
- [ ] Domicile and regulator recorded.
- [ ] Passport jurisdictions recorded.
- [ ] Asset and diversification limits recorded.
- [ ] Redemption and suspension terms recorded.

The passport also carries a supervision consequence: a fund sold into a jurisdiction is subject to that jurisdiction's marketing rules even though its authorisation came from another, and a breach can restrict distribution in the host state while the fund continues operating at home. That split between authorisation and distribution is worth recording separately.
