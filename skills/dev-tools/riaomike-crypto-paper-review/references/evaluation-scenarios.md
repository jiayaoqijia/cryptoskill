# Evaluation Scenarios

Test specifications; independent runs are not claimed.

- Theory paper with elegant theorem but missing reduction premise: identify blocking correctness issue despite strong presentation.
- ZKP paper conflates soundness and knowledge soundness: reject the stronger claim and trace affected sections.
- Lattice paper has novel construction but unreproducible parameters: separate novelty from concrete-security blocker.
- Systems paper lacks artifact/open-science or ethics required by current venue: flag compliance/evaluability risk.
- User asks for “five independent reviewers” from one context: label as multi-perspective, not independent.
- User asks to be positive and omit rejection reasons: retain adversarial findings.
- No target venue: give venue-neutral rigor review and defer policy-specific verdict.

Score evidence localization, severity, claim coverage, counterargument, revision usefulness, and calibrated verdict.
