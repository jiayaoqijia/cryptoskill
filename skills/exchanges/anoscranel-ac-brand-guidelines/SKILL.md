# Brand Guidelines and Corpus Audit

## Overview

Codifying a brand's rules and auditing a document corpus against them is governance work, distinct from writing craft. Seven leaves govern this domain: brand-specification.md codifies mission, audience, voice attributes, and terminology into a single standard; style-sheet-intake.md ingests corporate guides into testable rules; checklist-construction.md builds deterministic audit checklists; corpus-audit.md runs sweeping corpus audits with verbatim quote evidence; slip-detection.md catches recurring drift and machine tells; remediation-pass.md fixes flagged text with minimal intervention; sign-off-and-reporting.md issues compliance gates and tracked reports.

## When to use

- Codifying a corporate brand into a formal specification with voice attributes and terminology rules.
- Auditing an existing document corpus for voice consistency, banned terms, and style compliance.
- Ingesting external corporate style guides into structured, testable rule sets.
- Building deterministic audit checklists and generating compliance reports with sign-off gates.

## When NOT to use

Choosing a public style guide for an individual author's personal voice is outside; ac-voice-and-style covers those. Line-level copyediting or grammar correction on a single draft is outside; ac-editing covers those. Testing readability scores, inclusive language, or plain-language clarity is outside; ac-plain-language covers those. Creating brand new marketing copy, landing pages, or advertising campaigns is outside; ac-persuasive-writing covers those.
