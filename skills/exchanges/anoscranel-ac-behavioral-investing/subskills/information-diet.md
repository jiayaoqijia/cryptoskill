# Information diet

## Standard

Information is not free. Each price check is an opportunity to react, and each reaction is a chance to convert a long-horizon plan into a series of short-horizon impulses. The research on myopic loss aversion suggests that more frequent feedback makes investors more risk-averse and worse at holding risky assets, which means checking frequency is a policy variable, not a habit.

The diet has two dimensions: frequency and source quality. Frequency is set by the policy: a long-horizon portfolio does not need intraday prices, and reading them creates the stimulus for action. Source quality is set by lineage discipline: a claim that reaches you through three aggregators has one origin.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://www.jstor.org/stable/1914185>.

## Decision rules

- Checking frequency is stated in the policy and matches the decision horizon.
- Price checks are separated from decision sessions; a check is not a decision.
- News is read for scheduled events and primary documents, not for ambient commentary.
- Every repeated statistic is traced to its origin before it is used.
- Social feeds are treated as sentiment evidence, not as information.
- Any change to checking frequency is a policy change and is dated.

## Procedure

1. State the decision horizon and the frequency of checks that matches it.
2. Separate the check ritual from the decision session.
3. List the sources that are allowed to trigger a decision, and the ones that are not.
4. Build a scheduled-events calendar from primary sources.
5. Trace repeated statistics to their origin.
6. Record the diet in the policy and review it annually.

## Anti-patterns

- Checking prices hourly on a multi-year portfolio.
- Treating a social feed as a source of facts.
- Letting a notification set the agenda.
- Reading summaries of summaries.
- Changing the checking frequency during a drawdown.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Check ritual separated from decision sessions.
- [ ] Allowed and disallowed sources listed.
