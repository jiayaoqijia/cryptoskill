## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill includes examples for persistent publication, on-chain writes, payments, unpinned package installs, and agent-supplied URLs without enough safety guidance.

Mitigation: Review snippets before use, prefer testnet examples, pin SDK versions in isolated environments, require explicit wallet and payment confirmation, avoid publishing secrets or internal endpoints to IPFS, and validate or proxy agent-supplied URLs before server-side interaction.
