## Purpose
Create a clear proposal scope draft without inventing pricing or commitments.

## Use when
- Converting discovery insights into a scoped proposal outline.
- Need optional tiers and assumption tracking.

## Required inputs
- Confirmed objectives and deliverables.
- Constraints, timeline, and dependencies.
- Any approved pricing guidance (if available).

## Safety/authority
- Draft-only by default; no contract execution.
- Do not set fixed pricing unless provided by user/approved context.
- No guaranteed results or legal assurances.

## Workflow
1. Define outcomes and in-scope deliverables.
2. List assumptions, exclusions, and dependencies.
3. Draft timeline/milestones and optional tiers.
4. Generate decision/approval questions for stakeholder sign-off.

## Output format
```
- Scope summary
- In scope / Out of scope
- Assumptions + dependencies
- Milestones
- Optional tiers
- Approval questions
```

## Quality checks
- Scope avoids unstated commitments.
- Pricing placeholders used when needed.
- Approval questions resolve major ambiguity.
