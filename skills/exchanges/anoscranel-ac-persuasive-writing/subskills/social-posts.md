# Social posts

## Standard

- **The hook decides everything.** It drives thread-click rate. Seven patterns recur: a specific number, a contrarian claim, confession plus pivot, a numbered promise, a question, a specific outcome with mystery, a counter-intuitive opening (<https://mediafa.st/x-engagement-thread-hooks-2026>).
- **Keep hooks under 200 characters.** Early velocity — about 20 reposts, 40 replies, 150 likes in 30 minutes — triggers secondary distribution. ⚠️ Practitioner benchmarks, not platform-published rules.
- **Each post must feel incomplete alone.** Every line opens a small question the next answers while a bigger one looms. A thin payoff triggers backlash.
- **Platform limits are hard constraints.** X: 280 characters, links counting 23; LinkedIn: 3,000 characters; Bluesky: 300 graphemes; Threads: 500 characters (<https://github.com/marian-kamenistak/linkedin-post-writing-skill>; https://help.x.com/en/using-x/types-of-posts; https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a528176).
- **Voice calibration beats drafting from nothing.** Feed 5–10 of the author's best posts and extract tone, sentence patterns, signature phrases and banned phrases. Do this before generating.
- **Repurposing is re-shaping, never shortening.**
 A blog section becomes a carousel, a thread, a video script — genuinely different shapes from the richest source material.

## Decision rules

1. Write the hook last, after the payoff is known. A hook that promises more than the thread delivers fails.
2. Front-load the stakes: why it matters, the cost of being wrong.
3. Use the customer's words, not the brand's. The landscape figures indicator-derived classification, so describe, do not assert.
4. One idea per post; the thread carries the depth.
5. Keep within the platform limit, and treat links as costing 23 characters on X.
6. Post the same idea on 3–7 days' delay, reformatted per platform, never pasted.
7. Cross-post to forums with numbers, personal context and no numbering.

## Procedure

1. Pick the one richest source: one finding, one story, one number.
2. Write the payoff first — the claim the reader keeps.
3. Choose the hook pattern that fits the payoff; draft five hooks and keep the one that opens a real gap.
4. Build the arc: hook → stakes → insight 1 → insight 2 → best insight plus payoff and call to action.
5. Trim every post to its platform limit; count link characters.
6. Calibrate the voice against 5–10 reference posts; strike the banned phrases.
7. Verify every number and quote against its source record.
8. Cut any post that only restates the previous one. **The human posts.**

## Anti-patterns

- "Here is a thread about productivity" — a label where a hook belongs.
- A thread whose first post gives everything away.
- Uniform sentence length and rhythm across all posts.
- A pasted cross-post that keeps the original platform's numbering.
- Engagement or follower metrics written as if they were outcomes.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Hook under 200 characters, opening a real gap.
- [ ] Stakes stated before the insights.
- [ ] Platform limits respected, link characters counted.
- [ ] Every number and quote verified.
- [ ] Voice calibrated to reference posts; banned phrases struck.
- [ ] Each post earns its place in the arc.
- [ ] **Human-gated:** posted by a named person.
