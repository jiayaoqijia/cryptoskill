# Crypto Market Intelligence — Community, News and Catalysts

Purpose: capture the narrative and information layer without confusing it with evidence. Social and news are **signals** for positioning and timing; they are not proof of value, revenue or safety.

## Source map (ranked)

1. Primary project channels: blog, docs, governance forum, GitHub releases, status page, official dashboards.
2. Independent research: SubnetRadar, Taosis, TaoMarketCap, Messari, Delphi, block explorers, Dune dashboards; label each.
3. Trade press: The Block, CoinDesk, Blockworks, TAO Daily, sector newsletters; check author, date and whether the piece is independent or sponsored.
4. Social: X, Discord, Telegram, Reddit, Farcaster; discovery and sentiment only.
5. Search/public social search: discovery only; never cite a snippet as if the underlying page was read.

## Metrics

| Layer | Metric | What it tells | How to read it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community size | X followers, Discord members/online, Telegram members, unique posters | Reach and attention | Size without unique posters is weak; check engagement ratio |
| Engagement | Replies/reposts per post, message velocity, question quality | Whether people care | Bursts around price events are not durable interest |
| Developer | GitHub stars/forks, contributors, commits 90d, open issues, issue response time, releases, docs freshness | Execution capacity | Stars without commits/issues are marketing |
| Governance | Proposals, turnout, quorum, contentious votes, timelock, execution | Real control | Proposals without turnout are decorative |
| News | Tier-1 vs trade vs project posts; independent confirmations; negative coverage | Information flow | A story repeating the press release is one source |
| Narrative | Keywords, mindshare, competitor mentions, influencer coverage | What the market believes | Label as positioning, not value |
| Catalysts | Dated events, status, expected impact, source | What can re-rate the asset | Unknown dates are not catalysts |

## Interpretation rules

- Growth in followers without growth in unique posters, developers or paying users is weak evidence.
- Sudden follower spikes before a token event, generic replies and low engagement suggest paid or bot activity.
- GitHub stars without commits, issues or releases are marketing; check the contribution graph and issue response.
- Governance proposals without turnout or binding execution are decorative; check quorum, timelock and on-chain execution.
- News that only repeats the project's announcement is not independent confirmation.
- Sentiment extremes can be contrarian; label them as positioning, not as intrinsic value.
- Community criticism is useful evidence of unresolved questions; do not dismiss it because the tone is negative.

## Red flags

- Anonymous team, no legal entity, no audit and no incident history.
- Discord/Telegram moderation silence; critical questions unanswered or deleted.
- GitHub issue backlog with no response; no commits in 90 days; docs stale.
- Governance forum inactive; owner-controlled parameters; no timelock.
- News flow all paid/PR; no independent coverage; inconsistent dates.
- Follower spike immediately before a listing, unlock or paid promotion.

## Reporting

- Standard mode: one table plus 3–5 bullets. Deep mode: one page with the table, catalyst calendar and interpretation.
- Label every item: `verified`, `company claim`, `media report`, `social signal`, `unknown`.
- Never use social sentiment as a valuation input. Use it to understand positioning, narrative and near-term catalysts.
- Record the retrieval date for every metric; community numbers decay quickly.
