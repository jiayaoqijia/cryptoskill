# Qualitative record

## Standard

The qualitative synthesis record holds what a reader needs to judge the synthesis's honesty: the chosen approach and the reason it was chosen, the coding decisions and their audit table, the trace from every theme down to source passages, the appraisal profiles of contributing studies, the refutational cases and their explanations, and the per-finding confidence ratings with their domain reasons. It is the reconstruction kit: with the record and the primary studies, another analyst should be able to see how every theme was built and disagree with any step at the step where they disagree.

Its second function is guarding against the drift that interpretive work invites. Because the synthesist's judgement is part of the method, the record is the only place where that judgement is visible — which codes were created and when, which were merged, which themes were dropped and why, and where an analytical step went beyond the descriptive one. A record that shows the work makes the interpretation challengeable; a record that shows only the result makes it merely assertive.

Primary sources: <https://bmcmedresmethodol.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2288-8-45> and <https://casp-uk.net/casp-tools-checklists/>.

## Decision rules

- Record the approach, the reason for choosing it, and any amendments with dates.
- Keep the coding audit table: code, source study, page, exact text.
- Record every theme — kept and dropped — with its supporting codes.
- Attach appraisal profiles per study and confidence ratings per finding.
- Record refutational cases and their explanations or open status.
- Date any post-hoc change to themes or ratings as an amendment with its trigger.

## Procedure

1. Record the approach selection and the protocol reference.
2. Archive the coding audit table as built during coding.
3. Record descriptive and analytical themes with their code memberships.
4. Tabulate dropped themes and orphan codes with the reason for each.
5. Attach appraisal profiles and per-finding confidence records.
6. Version the whole record; date amendments.

## Anti-patterns

- A record showing themes without the codes beneath them.
- Dropping a theme from the record because it was dropped from the report.
- Reporting the approach without the reason it was chosen.
- Confidence levels without their domain reasons.
- Editing the audit table after the synthesis to match the final themes.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Approach and reason recorded.
- [ ] Coding audit table archived.
- [ ] Themes recorded with code memberships.
- [ ] Dropped themes and orphans recorded with reasons.
- [ ] Appraisal and confidence records attached.
- [ ] Record versioned, amendments dated.
