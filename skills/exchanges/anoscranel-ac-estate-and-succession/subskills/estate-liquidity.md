# Estate liquidity

## Standard

An estate faces costs before it distributes anything: taxes, professional fees, debts, and administration expenses. Those costs are paid in cash and often on a timetable the estate does not control. A portfolio that is entirely in illiquid assets — a private business, a property, a locked position — can force a sale at a discount, and the discount is a transfer of value away from the beneficiaries.

The analysis is a simple mismatch test: cash and near-cash available, against the estimated costs and their timing. Where the mismatch is material, the record names the sources of liquidity that could be tapped and the cost of each, and the professional question of how to structure them.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://www.irs.gov/>.

## Decision rules

- Estimate the estate's costs and their timing, with sources or stated judgement.
- List the assets by liquidity: cash, marketable securities, and illiquid holdings.
- Compare cash and near-cash with the estimated costs.
- Where the mismatch is material, name the liquidity sources and their costs.
- Record which assets would be sold first and the likely discount.
- Flag the structure question for a professional rather than solving it.

## Procedure

1. Estimate costs: taxes, fees, debts, and administration.
2. Classify assets by liquidity.
3. Compute the coverage of costs by cash and near-cash.
4. Identify the first assets that would be sold and the expected discount.
5. Record liquidity sources and their costs.
6. Write the findings and the open questions.

## Anti-patterns

- Assuming the estate can sell assets at quoted prices.
- Ignoring the timing of costs.
- Treating retirement accounts as immediately liquid.
- Solving a structure question without a professional.
- Estimating costs without recording the basis.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Costs estimated with basis recorded.
- [ ] Assets classified by liquidity.
- [ ] Coverage computed.
- [ ] First-sale assets and discounts identified.
- [ ] Liquidity sources and costs recorded.
- [ ] Open questions listed.
