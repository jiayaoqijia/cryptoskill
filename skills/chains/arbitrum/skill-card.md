## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Bridge or wallet guidance can become stale or be applied on the wrong network.

Mitigation: Verify current details against official Arbitrum and wallet sources before moving funds.

Risk: Transfers to an incorrect network or address can be hard to recover.

Mitigation: Confirm the selected network and recipient address and use a small test transfer first.

Risk: Third-party bridges can add liquidity, fee, and counterparty risk.

Mitigation: Use the official bridge for larger transfers and evaluate third-party bridge risk before use.
