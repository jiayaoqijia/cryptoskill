# Desk Routine

## Standard

- **Mintzberg, _The Nature of Managerial Work_ (1973)**: structured operational routines prevent decision fatigue and maintain systematic control. <https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/mono/10.4324/9780203808450/nature-managerial-work-henry-mintzberg>
- **Allison, _Essence of Decision_ (1971)**: rigorous organizational procedures prevent ad-hoc improvisation from corrupting decisions. <https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/essence-of-decision-explaining-the-cuban-missile-crisis/P200000003507> ⚠️ cited without URL.
- **Deming, _Out of the Crisis_ (1982)**: continuous improvement requires a disciplined cycle of planning, execution, and review. <https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262541152/>
- **Senge, _The Fifth Discipline_ (1990)**: systemic learning depends on regular reflection cadences evaluating feedback loops. <https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/162816/the-fifth-discipline-by-peter-senge/>
- ⚠️ Practitioner heuristic: a solo operator must separate analyst and risk manager personas.

## Decision rules

1. The desk must operate on a strict rhythm: daily brief, active monitoring, and weekly audit.
2. The solo operator must maintain role separation: analyst proposes setups; risk manager audits risk budgets.
3. The daily brief must document book state, open exposures, aggregate risk, and volatility shifts.
4. The weekly review must compile journal entries, calculate compliance, and audit drawdown reserves.
5. Operational records are immutable; briefs and reviews are permanently archived in the permanent record.
6. Trading is prohibited outside scheduled windows or when daily preparation is bypassed.
7. ⚠️ House: operating without completing the daily brief is an unmonitored risk session.

## Procedure

1. **Daily Briefing:** Review macro environment and verify aggregate open risk and drawdown reserves.
2. **Analyst Phase:** Formulate market observations and draft pre-trade memos without ordering.
3. **Risk Manager Phase:** Audit drafted memos against risk budget and invalidation rules; approve or reject.
4. **Execution Phase:** Execute approved memos strictly within sizing and stop parameters.
5. **Monitoring Phase:** Continuously track open positions against invalidation triggers.
6. **Weekly Review:** Aggregate closed trades, calculate compliance, and update the journal.
7. **Record Preservation:** Commit all artifacts to the version-controlled audit repository.

## Anti-patterns

- **Continuous trading.** Operating without scheduled pauses, leading to fatigue. Fix: enforce mandatory desk closure.
- **Blending analyst and risk roles.** Approving trades without independent risk review. Fix: execute formal role switches.
- **Skipping the daily brief.** Opening positions without reviewing book state. Fix: make briefs non-negotiable.
- **Transient record keeping.** Storing notes in ephemeral scratchpads. Fix: enforce permanent-record storage first.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Daily brief completed and archived before trade execution.
- [ ] Analyst and risk manager role separation executed for every memo.
- [ ] Active positions monitored against invalidation dimensions.
- [ ] Weekly review compiled, auditing process adherence and reserves.
- [ ] All records permanently committed to the audit trail.
