# Proposal Writing

## Overview

Three leaves cover documents a decision-maker scores. proposals.md writes RFP responses: compliance matrix, win themes, executive summary. grants.md writes funder-scored narratives: significance, innovation, approach. applications.md writes selector-scored documents: cover letters, personal statements, admissions essays.

## When to use

- Answering an RFP, tender or call for tender.
- Writing a grant proposal section or specific-aims page.
- Writing a cover letter, personal statement, statement of purpose or admissions essay.

## When not to use

- Persuasive copy aimed at a general audience — outside this skill.
- The one-ask email or the standalone memo — outside this skill.
- A press statement — outside this skill.
