## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill uses wallet-controlling private keys and saves Farcaster credentials as plaintext by default. <br>
Mitigation: Use a fresh low-balance wallet, prefer the no-save option or secure secret storage, inspect the external Node source before use, and avoid committing credential files. <br>
