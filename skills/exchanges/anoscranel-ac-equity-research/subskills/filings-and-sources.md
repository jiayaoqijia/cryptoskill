# Filings and sources

## Standard

Everything in equity research traces back to a document. In the United States those documents live in the regulator's full-text filing system; in other jurisdictions they live in equivalent registries. The research discipline is knowing which filing answers which question, and refusing to accept a summary — including a broker's summary — when the original is available.

The four filings that carry most of the information are the annual report, the quarterly report, the current report of material events, and the proxy or ownership filings. Each has a different purpose, and confusing them produces the common error of treating a press release as a financial statement.

Primary sources: <https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/> and <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing>.

## Decision rules

- Every quantitative claim cites a filing, a line, and a period.
- Annual report before quarterly: the annual carries audited statements and the risk disclosures.
- Current reports of material events are read within a day of appearance; they are where surprises live.
- Ownership and proxy filings are the source for insider holdings and compensation, not news articles.
- Where the jurisdiction's registry is not the one you know, find the local equivalent before proceeding.
- If a number cannot be traced to a filing, record it as unverified.

## Procedure

1. Identify the company's home regulator and its filing registry.
2. Pull the latest annual report, the last four quarterly reports, and all material-event filings for the period.
3. Read the annual report's business description, risk factors, and audited statements before any summary.
4. Note the auditor, the fiscal year end, and any change of either.
5. Build a source index: claim, document, section, date.
6. Record any figure that came from a secondary source and mark it as such.

## Anti-patterns

- Reading a summary and citing it as the filing.
- Skipping risk factors because they are long and repetitive.
- Treating a press release as an audited statement.
- Using a screen's numbers without tracing them.
- Assuming the same filing regime across jurisdictions.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Regulator and registry identified.
- [ ] Annual report, four quarters, and material events pulled.
- [ ] Business description, risk factors, and audited statements read.
- [ ] Auditor and fiscal year recorded.
- [ ] Source index built with document and section references.
- [ ] Secondary-source figures marked.
