## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: BNB Chain details such as network names, RPC endpoints, contract addresses, bridge reputation, and fees can become stale or be spoofed.

Mitigation: Independently verify those details from official sources before acting, and test transfers with small amounts first.

Risk: Wallet or transaction guidance can be misused to expose keys or authorize malicious contracts.

Mitigation: Never share private keys or seed phrases, verify contract addresses, and revoke unused approvals.
