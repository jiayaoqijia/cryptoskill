# Nominal Group

## Standard

- **Delbecq & Van de Ven (1971)**: the nominal group technique sequences silent idea-writing, round-robin listing, a clarification pass with no debate, and independent ranking, then aggregates. <https://doi.org/10.1177/002188637100700404>
- **Delbecq, Van de Ven & Gustafson (1975)**: group techniques for program planning; the clarification-only pass keeps ranking independent of a speaker's status. <https://doi.org/10.1080/00131727509336471>
- Independent ranking before aggregation is what prevents first-speaker anchoring.

## Decision rules

1. If the first person to speak sets the ranking, the process failed; re-rank in writing.
2. Clarification may only answer "what do you mean", never "is it good".
3. Ranking is written and independent before any aggregation or discussion.
4. If the group cannot state the ranking criterion, fix that before ranking.
5. Ties are broken by a second vote on the tied items only.

## Procedure

1. Present the question and the ranking criterion to the whole group.
2. Silent generation: each person writes their own ideas alone for 5 to 10 minutes.
3. Round-robin capture: one idea per person per round, recorded verbatim, no comment, until the list is exhausted.
4. Clarification pass: ask only for meaning; the author answers; no evaluation.
5. Independent ranking: each person ranks or scores the full list in writing.
6. Aggregate: sum or average the scores, then order the list.
7. Read back the top cluster and confirm it reflects the group, not the loudest voice.

## Anti-patterns

- **First-speaker dominance.** The first opinion becomes the group's. Fix: silent generation and written ranking.
- **Premature clustering.** Ideas are merged into the first similar one before they are understood. Fix: keep items distinct through clarification; merge only after ranking.
- **Clarification that argues.** "What do you mean" turns into "that won't work". Fix: enforce the meaning-only rule.
- **Ranking before capture is complete.** Items left out skew the vote. Fix: exhaust the list first.
- **Aggregation without a criterion.** Rankings chase nothing. Fix: state the criterion up front.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Silent generation happened before any shared list.
- [ ] Round-robin capture was verbatim and comment-free.
- [ ] Clarification stayed meaning-only.
- [ ] Ranking was written and independent before aggregation.
- [ ] The aggregated shortlist was read back and confirmed.
