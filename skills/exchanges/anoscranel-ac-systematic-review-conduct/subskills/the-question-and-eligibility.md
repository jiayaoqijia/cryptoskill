# The question and eligibility

## Standard

The review question sets a boundary, and the boundary decides everything after it. Population, intervention, comparison, outcome — PICO for intervention questions, with variants for diagnostic, prognostic, and qualitative reviews — plus the study designs that qualify. A question built as "what is known about X" is not a review question; it is a topic, and topics have no boundary to be reproducible against.

Eligibility criteria are the operational form of the question: the inclusion and exclusion rules that decide, before any title is read, whether a record can enter. Written after the search, they become a mirror of whatever was found. Written before, they are a commitment that the review can break — and a review that excludes studies its criteria actually admit is misreported even if every included study is sound.

Primary sources: <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook> and <https://prisma-statement.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Frame the question as PICO or its named variant, with each component explicit.
- State the study designs that qualify, not only the population and intervention.
- Fix inclusion and exclusion criteria before the first search, with the date-stamped protocol.
- Define what happens to a study that meets every criterion except one — in, out, or a named pending category.
- State the outcomes of interest in advance, including which are primary.
- Write the criteria so another team could apply them to the same records and reach the same set.

## Procedure

1. Draft the question in PICO form and name the review type.
2. Convert each component into an eligibility rule.
3. Decide the handling of partial matches and of studies reporting subsets.
4. Fix the primary and secondary outcomes.
5. Date-stamp the criteria before any search runs.
6. Test the criteria against three known studies — one clearly in, one clearly out, one borderline — and refine before sealing.

## Anti-patterns

- A question with no exclusion rule, so everything is admissible.
- Criteria drafted after scanning the results.
- Outcomes added once a striking result appears in an abstract.
- "Relevant studies" as an inclusion criterion.
- Changing a criterion silently after screening has begun.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Question framed in PICO or a named variant.
- [ ] Designs and outcomes fixed in advance.
- [ ] Inclusion and exclusion rules written and date-stamped.
- [ ] Partial-match handling defined.
- [ ] Borderline treatment defined.
- [ ] Criteria tested on known in/out/borderline studies.
