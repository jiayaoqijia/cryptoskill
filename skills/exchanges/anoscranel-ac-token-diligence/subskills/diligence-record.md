# Diligence record

## Standard

The record is the deliverable. A diligence that lives in memory cannot be reviewed, cannot be audited after a loss, and cannot tell you later whether you were wrong about the facts or wrong about the inference. The record separates those two, because they teach different lessons.

A usable record has five parts: what was checked, what was found with sources and dates, what remains unknown, the confidence level, and the decision with its review date. It is written before exposure and amended only by appending a dated note, never by rewriting the original finding.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://osf.io/prereg/>.

## Decision rules

- Every finding carries a source, a date, and a confidence level (high, medium, low, unknown).
- Unknowns are listed as a section, not omitted; an empty unknowns section is itself a claim that must be justified.
- The decision section states what was decided and on which findings, not what is hoped.
- The record is append-only: later changes are dated amendments, never silent edits.
- A review date is mandatory and tied to the next material event (unlock, governance change, treasury report).
- If the red-flag list fired, the record states that and stops.

## Procedure

1. Open the record with the token, date, and the question being answered.
2. Fill the findings table from the six diligence leaves, each row with source, date, confidence.
3. Write the unknowns section explicitly.
4. State the decision and the findings it rests on.
5. Set the review date and the trigger that forces an early re-check.
6. File the record where the trade journal will reference it, and reference it from the trade memo if exposure follows.

## Anti-patterns

- Recording conclusions without the evidence behind them.
- Rewriting a record after the fact so the original view disappears.
- Omitting unknowns to make the record look complete.
- Setting a review date with no trigger behind it.
- Treating the record as a recommendation for anyone other than its author.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Token, date, and the question stated.
- [ ] Findings table with source, date, and confidence per row.
- [ ] Unknowns listed explicitly.
- [ ] Decision stated with the findings it rests on.
- [ ] Review date and early-recheck trigger set.
- [ ] Record filed and referenced from the trade memo where exposure follows.
