# Macro narratives

## Standard

Every market has a prevailing story that explains recent moves and implies the next ones. The story is useful as a summary and dangerous as a forecast: it is fitted to the past, it selects the evidence that supports it, and it usually survives until a data point contradicts it decisively.

The discipline is to state the narrative explicitly, identify the evidence it rests on, and write the observation that would retire it. A narrative you cannot falsify is a mood, and moods are what the portfolio ends up trading when no one writes them down.

Primary sources: <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing> and <https://fred.stlouisfed.org/>.

## Decision rules

- State the narrative in one sentence, attributed to its holders rather than to the world.
- List the evidence it cites and the evidence it ignores.
- Write the observation that would retire it.
- Record the narrative's age and what it replaced.
- Do not let a narrative justify a position without the position's own evidence.
- Review the narrative when its retiring observation appears, not when it becomes inconvenient.

## Procedure

1. Write the narrative in one sentence.
2. List its supporting evidence.
3. List the evidence it omits.
4. Write its retirement condition.
5. Record when it began and what it replaced.
6. Review on the retirement condition or the scheduled date.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating the narrative as the data.
- Selecting evidence after the fact.
- Letting a narrative substitute for a position's own thesis.
- Keeping a narrative past its retirement condition.
- Attributing the narrative to the market rather than to its holders.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Narrative stated in one sentence.
- [ ] Supporting evidence listed.
- [ ] Omitted evidence listed.
- [ ] Retirement condition written.
- [ ] Age and predecessor recorded.
- [ ] Review scheduled.

The useful posture is to hold the narrative as the market's working hypothesis rather than as your own conclusion. That framing makes it possible to describe what the narrative implies without adopting it, which is what allows a position to be sized against it rather than from it.

Narratives also have a social function that is worth naming: they coordinate positioning, which is why they persist past their evidence. Knowing that the narrative is what others are trading on is different from believing it, and the difference is the whole discipline.
