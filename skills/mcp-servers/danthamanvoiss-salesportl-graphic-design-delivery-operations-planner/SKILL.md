## Purpose
Design dependable graphic design services delivery SOPs with role ownership, QA checkpoints, and feedback loops.

## Use when
- You are operating inside `SalesPortl-Graphic-Design-Skills-Suite`.
- You need graphic design services execution quality for graphic design agencies, freelance studios, and in-house brand teams.
- You want repeatable, improving workflows that learn from each run via context + memory updates.

## Industry fit: micro-niche variants
- brand identity studios
- social creative studios
- packaging-focused designers

## Standard operating patterns (SOP)
- creative brief standardization
- asset review/approval loops
- brand consistency and revision control

## SalesPortl platform tooling focus (if available in workspace)
- campaign creative packaging for launch sequences
- click tracking mapping for creative CTAs
- workflow nodes for concept→review→delivery

## Required inputs
- Business objective and requested deliverable
- Current context quality (none/partial/verified)
- Business model, offer mix, and target customer profile
- Authority boundary for external actions (draft-only unless approved)
- Available SalesPortl platform features in this workspace

## Workflow
1. Convert sold scope into implementation milestones with role ownership.
2. Map SOP gates for kickoff, execution, QA, and closeout.
3. Define exceptions/escalations and contingency handling for common operational failures.
4. Add consistency loop: compare actual execution vs SOP and patch gaps each cycle.

## Platform execution checklist
- Campaign orchestration nodes
- Click-tracking and attribution configuration
- Workflow/automation nodes
- Custom agent launch and role-specialized sub-agents
- CRM pipeline and lead stage automation
- Calendar, booking, and follow-up automations
- Website/page/app builder flows (where available)
