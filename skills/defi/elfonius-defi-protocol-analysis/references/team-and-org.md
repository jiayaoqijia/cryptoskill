# Team, Funding and Organization

## Who is accountable?

DeFi protocols often have several layers:

- founding team / core contributors;
- foundation or legal entity;
- DAO/governance;
- curator or risk manager;
- multisig signers / security council;
- auditors, keepers and service providers.

Name the person or entity behind each. “The community” is not an accountable operator.

## Team review

- Founders and core contributors: relevant experience, shipped products, prior failures.
- Verified track record: GitHub commits, audits, talks, prior protocol exits, public references.
- Anonymous versus doxxed; if anonymous, what compensating evidence exists?
- Key-person dependency: who maintains the oracle, risk engine, front end or treasury?
- Hiring and retention: open roles, contributor concentration, turnover.
- Security culture: incident response, postmortems, disclosure, bug bounty.
- Conflicts: team wallets, affiliated curator/market/oracle, side companies, market-making.
- Legal/regulatory history: enforcement, sanctions, disputes, failed entities.

## Funding and runway

| Item | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Equity/token cap table | Founders, VCs, treasury, community, advisors |
| Rounds and valuation | Date, amount, price, rights, dilution |
| Treasury | Stablecoins, native token, other assets; who controls it |
| Burn/runway | Monthly spend, stablecoin runway, token-funded runway |
| Revenue | Real revenue vs grants/incentives |
| Related entities | Foundation, labs, market maker, curator, service company |

Separate **protocol treasury** (user/DAO funds) from **company treasury** (team funds). A buyback funded by the protocol treasury is not the same as a team buyback.

## Governance and control

- Who appoints/removes the security council or multisig signers?
- Are signers independent of the team and of each other?
- Can the team change the rules without a token vote?
- Does the foundation control the front end, domain, RPC or treasury?
- Are contributor grants/vesting aligned with the protocol's horizon?

## Red flags

- Anonymous team with custody of user funds and no independent attestation.
- A single person controls upgrades, oracle and treasury.
- “Doxxed” profiles with no verifiable shipping history.
- Advisors/investors with a history of failed or exploited projects.
- Treasury runway measured in the protocol's own token.
- Related-party market maker/curator/oracle with no disclosure.
- Team can change fees, collateral or withdrawal terms without a timelock.
- High contributor turnover or a single maintainer for critical infrastructure.

## Checklist

- [ ] Founders and core contributors identified; claims verified.
- [ ] Key-person and hiring risks assessed.
- [ ] Funding, cap table and runway reconciled.
- [ ] Protocol treasury vs company treasury separated.
- [ ] Signer independence and conflicts checked.
- [ ] Team wallets and related entities labelled.
- [ ] Incident/disclosure history reviewed.
