## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Real AVAX transfers, bridge actions, and token approvals can be irreversible or costly if the chain, address format, token contract, bridge interface, fees, or wallet support are wrong.

Mitigation: Verify all transaction details against official sources and test with a small amount before moving meaningful funds.

Risk: Cross-chain and subnet activity can involve wrapped assets, third-party bridges, bridge delays, and different security assumptions.

Mitigation: Prefer official wallet and bridge paths where appropriate, confirm asset representations and withdrawal timing, and review subnet-specific bridge and validator assumptions.
