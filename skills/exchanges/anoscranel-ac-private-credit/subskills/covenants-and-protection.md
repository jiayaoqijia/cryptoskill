# Covenants and protection

## Standard

A covenant is a right to act before a payment is missed. A maintenance covenant requires the borrower to keep a ratio above a threshold and is tested periodically, so a breach gives the lender a seat at the table early. An incurrence covenant is tested only when the borrower takes an action, such as raising debt or paying a dividend, so it constrains behaviour without providing an early warning.

Covenant-lite lending removes or loosens maintenance tests, which is why it became popular with sponsors and why it changes the lender's position. Without a maintenance test, the lender's first signal is a default or a restructuring rather than a breach, and the recovery process starts later, with less information and less leverage. The gap between the protections assumed and the protections written is where the losses concentrate.

Primary sources: <https://www.federalreserve.gov/> and <https://www.cfainstitute.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Record whether the facility has maintenance covenants, and the levels and test dates.
- Record the incurrence covenants and the baskets they permit.
- Record the cure rights and the equity cure provisions.
- Record the definition of the metric used in each test, since definitions move.
- Record the grace periods and the notice requirements.
- Record what the lender can do on breach, and how quickly.

## Procedure

1. List every covenant and classify it.
2. Record levels, definitions, and test frequency.
3. Record cure rights and equity cures.
4. Record the consequences of a breach.
5. Compare with the covenant package of comparable loans.
6. Write the protection assessment.

## Anti-patterns

- Reading a covenant list without the definitions.
- Treating an incurrence covenant as an early warning.
- Ignoring equity cure rights, which can undo a breach.
- Assuming a covenant-lite loan has no protections at all.
- Comparing covenant levels across loans with different definitions.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Covenants listed and classified.
- [ ] Levels and definitions recorded.
- [ ] Cure rights recorded.
- [ ] Breach consequences recorded.
- [ ] Comparison made.
- [ ] Assessment written.

The definition of the metric matters as much as its level: a leverage test that nets equity against debt, or that uses a run-rate rather than a trailing figure, can pass comfortably while the business deteriorates. Reading a covenant means reading the defined term and following it to the calculation schedule, because the number in the test is rarely the number in the financial statements.
