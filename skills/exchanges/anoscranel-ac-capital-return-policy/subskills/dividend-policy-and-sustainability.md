# Dividend policy and sustainability

## Standard

The payout ratio is the dividend divided by earnings, and it is the starting point rather than the answer. Earnings can be managed, capitalised, or inflated by working capital; free cash flow is the harder test, and the dividend divided by free cash flow is the coverage that actually constrains the payment. A company paying out ninety percent of accounting earnings and one hundred and twenty percent of free cash flow is funding the difference from its balance sheet.

The sustainability tests form a ladder: coverage from free cash flow, trend in that coverage, the trajectory of debt, the capital expenditure required to maintain the business, and the company's stated commitment to the dividend relative to other uses of cash. A cut usually follows a sequence in which coverage deteriorates for several periods while management reaffirms the policy, which is why the trend matters more than the level.

Primary sources: <https://www.sec.gov/investor> and <https://www.cfainstitute.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Record the dividend per share, the total paid, and the period's free cash flow.
- Compute coverage as free cash flow divided by dividends paid.
- Record the coverage trend over at least five periods.
- Record maintenance capital expenditure and its proportion of operating cash flow.
- Record the debt trajectory and any covenant that limits distributions.
- Record management's stated policy and whether it has changed form.

## Procedure

1. Collect dividends paid and free cash flow across periods.
2. Compute coverage and its trend.
3. Record maintenance capital needs.
4. Record debt and distribution covenants.
5. Record the stated policy and any recent change.
6. Write the sustainability assessment with the evidence.

## Anti-patterns

- Using earnings per share as the coverage denominator.
- Reading one period's coverage as the trend.
- Ignoring maintenance capital expenditure.
- Overlooking a covenant that restricts distributions.
- Treating a reaffirmed policy as evidence of capacity.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Dividends and free cash flow recorded.
- [ ] Coverage and trend computed.
- [ ] Maintenance capital recorded.
- [ ] Covenants recorded.
- [ ] Stated policy recorded.
- [ ] Assessment written with evidence.

The dividend's interaction with buybacks belongs in the same test: a company funding a dividend and a repurchase simultaneously is returning more than its free cash flow unless it is drawing on the balance sheet, and the combined payout ratio, not either figure alone, is the number that shows whether the policy is self-funding.
