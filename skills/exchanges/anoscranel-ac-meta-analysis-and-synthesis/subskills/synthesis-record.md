# Synthesis record

## Standard

The synthesis record is the lab notebook of the analysis: the measure, the model and the belief it encodes, the heterogeneity statistics, every asymmetry and moderator and sensitivity test with its result, and the certainty rating with its reasons. It exists because a meta-analysis produces far more numbers than any report can show, and the difference between the ones shown and the ones run is exactly where selective reporting hides.

Its discipline is completeness over narrative. Every analysis that was run appears, significant or not; every variant that moved the estimate is named; every model choice records the belief it encodes. The record is the place where a later analyst can reconstruct every step from the same inputs and check the study set against the conduct record - because a synthesis is only reproducible if the population of studies behind it is.

Primary sources: <https://training.cochrane.org/handbook/current/chapter-10> and <https://prisma-statement.org/>.

## Decision rules

- Record the effect measure, model, estimator, and the substantive belief each encodes.
- Tabulate every analysis run: primaries, sensitivities, moderators, asymmetry tests.
- Name the variants that moved the estimate; omit none.
- Cross-reference the study set to the conduct record's PRISMA counts.
- Record the certainty rating with per-domain reasons.
- Keep the record append-only; post-hoc edits are dated amendments with reasons.

## Procedure

1. Record measure, model, and estimators with their encoded beliefs.
2. Tabulate all analyses with results, significant and non-significant.
3. Mark the estimate-moving variants explicitly.
4. Reconcile the study count against the conduct record.
5. Attach the certainty rating and its domain judgements.
6. Version the record; date any amendment.

## Anti-patterns

- Recording only the analyses that appear in the report.
- Omitting a sensitivity that changed the conclusion.
- Editing the record after review comments, without dating the change.
- Keeping the analysis record apart from the conduct record it depends on.
- Stating a certainty rating without the domain judgements that produced it.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Measure, model, estimators recorded with beliefs.
- [ ] All analyses tabulated with results.
- [ ] Estimate-moving variants named.
- [ ] Study count reconciled to conduct.
- [ ] Certainty domains recorded.
- [ ] Record versioned and dated.

The record's final test is reconstruction: a second analyst given the conduct record and this synthesis record should reach the same numbers without asking a question. Every choice that a reconstruction would need — measure, model, estimator, moderator list, sensitivity set, rating reasons — is either in the record or the synthesis is not reproducible.
