# Evaluation Scenarios

Test specifications; independent runs are not claimed.

- Draft an IACR-style theory paper from complete theorem/proof/citation records: produce claim-evidence architecture and calibrated manuscript.
- Draft a systems-security paper with artifacts: include ethics/open-science, claim-to-artifact links, fair baselines, and reproducibility limits.
- User supplies an idea but no results: return research plan/outline, not a result-bearing paper.
- Novelty search is partial: forbid “first” and use bounded wording.
- Proof has an unresolved gap: expose it prominently; do not smooth it away in prose.
- Citation metadata is uncertain: keep citation unresolved rather than invent BibTeX.
- Venue page limit/policy may have changed: require live CFP verification.

Score evidence alignment, claim calibration, venue/ethics/artifact gates, and absence of fabricated content.
