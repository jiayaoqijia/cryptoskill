# Withdrawal ordering

## Standard

Accounts differ in how withdrawals are taxed, and the order in which they are drawn changes the total tax paid over a lifetime. Drawing from a taxable account first allows sheltered accounts to compound, but it can leave a large deferred balance that must be withdrawn later at a higher rate. Drawing from deferred accounts first uses up lower brackets while they are available, but it forgoes the compounding.

The interaction with required distributions and with other income makes the ordering a forecast rather than a rule. The record should state the order chosen, the brackets it assumes, and the events that would change it: a change in the tax rules, in other income, or in health. The honest framing is that the order is a plan to be reviewed, not a permanent instruction.

Primary sources: <https://www.irs.gov/> and <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing>.

## Decision rules

- Record the account types and their withdrawal tax treatment.
- Record the required distributions and their start dates.
- Record the brackets assumed and the income they are applied to.
- Record the chosen order and the reasoning.
- Record the effect of a large deferred balance on future brackets.
- Record the review triggers: rule change, income change, health change.

## Procedure

1. Classify accounts by withdrawal treatment.
2. Record required distributions and dates.
3. Assume a bracket path and record it.
4. Choose the order and record the reasoning.
5. Test the order against a higher and lower future income.
6. Set review triggers.

## Anti-patterns

- Applying a rule of thumb from another jurisdiction.
- Ignoring required distributions and their effect on later brackets.
- Assuming current brackets persist for decades.
- Ordering on tax alone while ignoring access needs.
- Treating the order as permanent.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Accounts classified.
- [ ] Required distributions recorded.
- [ ] Bracket path assumed and recorded.
- [ ] Order chosen with reasoning.
- [ ] Sensitivity tested.
- [ ] Review triggers set.

The ordering also interacts with the investment policy through asset location: an account scheduled to be drawn first should hold the assets whose sale is least costly, and a location plan made without reference to the withdrawal order can put the most tax-sensitive holdings in the account that must be sold earliest.
