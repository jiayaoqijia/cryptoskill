# Templates

Choose by the decision, not by the length you feel like writing.

| Template | Use | Typical length | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| `investment-screen-en.md` | Quick screen, first look, comparison | 1 page, 300–500 words | English |
| `investment-memo-en.md` | Standard investment memo | 3–5 pages | English |
| `investment-report-en.md` | Deep research / PDF report | 8–15 pages | English |
| `investment-committee-en.md` | One-page IC summary for a decision meeting | 1 page | English |
| `investment-analysis.md` | Evidence workpaper; not a user-facing deliverable | 20–30 pages | English |
| `investment-raporu-tr.md` | Turkish short note | 1–2 pages | Turkish |
| `investment-report-full-tr.md` | Turkish deep report | 8–15 pages | Turkish |
| `investment-committee-tr.md` | Turkish IC summary | 1 page | Turkish |

## Rules

- Fill every `{{placeholder}}`; delete the internal comment and any unused section before delivery.
- The screen is not permission to skip the gate. If a Tier 1 control was not run, say so in one line.
- The deep report always includes a one-page executive summary.
- Keep the workpaper separate: the report is the argument; the workpaper is the evidence.
- No empty tables, no internal checklist, no `{{...}}` left in the delivered file.
- Do not add a composite score. Report the dimensions separately and state the verdict conditionally.

The Turkish templates are retained because the skill originated in a Turkish workflow; the English templates are the default for this repository. The method is language-independent.
