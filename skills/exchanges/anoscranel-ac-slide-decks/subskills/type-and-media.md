# Type and Media

## Standard

- **Kawasaki, 30-point minimum** for pitch decks. <https://guykawasaki.com/the_102030_rule/> ⚠️ practitioner convention.
- **Projection floors**: no body text below about 24 points, headlines at 36 points or more. Kawasaki's 30-point minimum is the stricter pitch-deck variant: under 10/20/30, treat 30 points as the body floor. ⚠️ practitioner convention; the number moves with room size, not with a standard.
- **Reynolds, Zen restraint**: large type, generous whitespace, one focus per slide. <https://www.presentationzen.com/>
- **Duarte, slides as billboards**: every image earns its place as evidence or context. <https://www.duarte.com/slideology/>
- **Rule of thirds**: place headline, visual, and annotation on a three-by-three grid. ⚠️ design canon applied to slides by practitioner convention.

## Decision rules

1. If the room is large or the screen is small, raise the floor. Design for the back row.
2. Use sans-serif on projection. Keep serif for the printed leave-behind.
3. One image per slide. The image is evidence or context, never decoration.
4. Real over stock: a screenshot, product photo, or artifact beats generic stock every time.
5. Animation exists for sequenced builds that reveal the argument. Entrance effects and transitions do not survive review.
6. Axis labels and legends get back-row sizes too. A chart with 12-point axes fails the floor.
7. If an image needs a caption to explain why it is there, cut it or replace it.

## Procedure

1. For each slide, note the intended visual and its role: evidence or context.
2. Note the intended type sizes per level. Flag anything under the floor.
3. Mark any slide with two competing visuals and sequence them instead.
4. Replace every stock-photo note with a concrete artifact description.
5. List every planned animation. Delete all but progressive disclosure.
6. Check the layout order: headline, visual, annotation, placed on the thirds grid.
7. Check the chart chrome: axis labels, tick labels, and units sized for the back row.

## Anti-patterns

- Fourteen-point body text "because the room is small".
- Decorative stock imagery: handshakes, globes, chess pieces.
- Bouncing entrances, spinning transitions, any sound.
- A full-bleed photo behind paragraph text. Fix: crop to evidence or move text off.
- Clip art of any era. Fix: a real artifact or nothing.
- Everything centered, no grid. Fix: thirds.

## Done checklist

- [ ] No text below the projection floor, or the floor raised for the room.
- [ ] One visual per slide; real artifacts over stock.
- [ ] No entrance effects or transitions; builds only.
- [ ] Headline, visual, annotation placed on the thirds grid.
- [ ] Chart chrome sized for the back row.
- [ ] Every image earned its place.
