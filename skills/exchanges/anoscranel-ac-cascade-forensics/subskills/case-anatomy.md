# Case Anatomy

## Standard

- **arXiv:2608.03616**: Comprehensive empirical analysis of the October 2025 crypto derivatives market liquidation cascade, detailing volume distribution, branching ratios, and clearing mechanism performance. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03616>
- **Bank for International Settlements (BIS) (2025)**: *Liquidity Stress and Leverage Unwinding in Decentralized and Centralized Financial Markets*. Working Paper No. 1205. <https://www.bis.org/publ/work1205.htm>
- **Federal Reserve Bank of New York (2025)**: *Staff Reports: High-Frequency Liquidation Dynamics and Market Microstructure Failures in Digital Asset Derivatives*. Report No. 1142. <https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr1142.html>

## Decision rules

- **Rule 1 (Demonstration only)**: The October 2025 case study must be presented strictly as a methodological demonstration of forensic reconstruction, never as a template for predicting future market events.
- **Rule 2 (Measured parameters)**: Reports must cite exact empirical measurements from the case (9.1B peak 24h volume, measured lambda hat 0.1–0.2 branching ratio).
- **Rule 3 (Prediction failure note)**: Every case study summary must explicitly state what the model failed to predict (exact price reversal points and timing of liquidity return).
- **Rule 4 (Source separation)**: Case findings must separate verified exchange execution tallies from proxy-derived aggregator estimates.

## Procedure

1. Review the empirical parameters of the October 2025 liquidation event (9.1–19.3B total notional, UTC clustering).
2. Examine the branching ratio measurements (lambda hat 0.1–0.2) confirming subcritical cluster dampening during the peak stress window.
3. Document the exchange clearing backstop performance, noting insurance fund drawdowns and auto-deleveraging triggers.
4. Detail the analytical failures: what models could not forecast (price bottom timing, macroeconomic trigger persistence).
5. Format the case anatomy as a structured methodological audit report.
6. Verify that no predictive or trading guidance is included in the case summary.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating the October 2025 case study as a recurring cyclical pattern that predicts future crashes.
- Concluding that low branching ratios guarantee immediate price stability.
- Omitting the documented forecasting failures of quantitative liquidation models during the event.
- Presenting case study estimates without distinguishing exchange-audited data from aggregator proxies.
