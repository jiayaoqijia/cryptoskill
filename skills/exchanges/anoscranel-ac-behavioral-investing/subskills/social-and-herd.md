# Social and herd

## Standard

Consensus is comfortable and often wrong at the extremes. The mechanism is not stupidity: groups reward agreement, disagreement is socially costly, and a rising price makes the majority view feel confirmed. The investor's problem is that the same pressure that makes consensus pleasant makes it hard to hold a policy that the crowd has abandoned.

The tool is the isolation test: state the decision without reference to what others are doing, and see whether it still stands. If the argument requires "everyone is buying", the decision is social. If it stands on the policy and the evidence, the crowd is irrelevant.

Primary sources: <https://www.jstor.org/stable/1914185> and <https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing>.

## Decision rules

- Every discretionary decision passes the isolation test before it is executed.
- The number of people who agree is not evidence and is recorded as social information.
- Disagreement with consensus is recorded with the reasons, not suppressed.
- Sentiment readings are treated as context for others' positioning, never as a signal.
- A decision that changes after a conversation is re-tested in writing.
- Herd language ("everyone", "nobody", "the market thinks") is flagged in the journal.

## Procedure

1. Write the decision with no reference to other participants.
2. Check whether it survives; if not, record the social dependency.
3. Note the consensus and the reasons it might be wrong at this point.
4. Re-test after any conversation that changed the view.
5. Flag herd language in the journal entry.
6. Record the isolation-test result with the decision.

## Anti-patterns

- Treating agreement as confirmation.
- Suppressing a dissent because the room disagrees.
- Using sentiment as a timing signal.
- Changing a decision after a conversation without re-testing it.
- Reading forums as evidence of fundamentals.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Decision written without social reference.
- [ ] Isolation test result recorded.
- [ ] Consensus and its failure modes noted.
- [ ] Re-test after conversations done.
- [ ] Herd language flagged.
- [ ] Result recorded with the decision.

The isolation test is cheap and uncomfortable, which is how you know it is working. A decision that survives it earns the right to be executed regardless of what the room thinks; one that does not survive it is a social act and should be recorded as one.
