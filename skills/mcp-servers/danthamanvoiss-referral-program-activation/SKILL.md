## Purpose

Draft referral-program activation plan, partner messaging, and compliance-aware rollout checklist.

## Use when

- Launching or reactivating a referral motion with partners/affiliates.
- You need a structured rollout without changing payout or legal terms directly.

## Required inputs

- Current referral policy details and approved claims [client-provided].
- Target partner segments and communication channels.
- Program context from context/BRAND-CONTEXT.template.md and context/CLIENT-CONTEXT.template.md.

## Safety/authority

- Do not invent incentives, commission terms, or compliance approvals.
- No legal interpretations; flag legal/compliance review requirements.
- Output is activation draft and checklist only.

## Workflow

1. Define activation objective, audience tiers, and offer boundaries.
2. Draft outreach/collateral prompts for each partner segment.
3. Build enablement checklist (training assets, tracking, handoff, review cadence).
4. List approvals and dependencies before launch.

## Output format
```

- Activation plan summary
- Partner messaging matrix
- Enablement checklist
- Approval/dependency log
```

## Quality checks

- All partner-facing claims match supplied policy terms.
- Launch steps include explicit compliance and approval gates.
