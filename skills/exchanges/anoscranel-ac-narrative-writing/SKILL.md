# Narrative Writing

## Overview

Two leaves carry story craft. narrative-nonfiction.md puts a story arc into true writing: case stories, customer stories, long-form features. speech-script.md writes for the ear: the talk, the keynote outline, demo narration, the video or podcast script.

## When to use

- Structuring a true story: scene, arc, characters, evidence.
- Writing a talk script, keynote or demo narration.
- Drafting a video or podcast script.

## When not to use

- A breaking-news story or press announcement — outside this skill.
- A memo or report meant to be read silently and alone — outside this skill.
- The technical claim-evidence-warrant structure — outside this skill.
