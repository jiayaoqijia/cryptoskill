## Purpose
Create a practical 30-day content schedule tied to objectives and available channels.

## Use when
- Planning month-ahead publishing cadence.
- Aligning sales enablement and top-of-funnel content.

## Required inputs
- Business goals and campaign priorities.
- Approved channels and posting cadence constraints.
- Offer and audience context.

## Safety/authority
- Draft-only by default; no direct publishing actions.
- No invented facts, testimonials, or performance claims.
- Respect platform/community rules for proposed channels.

## Workflow
1. Map goals to content themes.
2. Create 30-day calendar by date/channel/format.
3. Attach CTA intent and required proof/assets.
4. Flag missing approvals/dependencies.

## Output format
```
- Monthly theme map
- Day-by-day calendar table
- Asset dependencies
- Approval checklist
```

## Quality checks
- Every item links to a business objective.
- Claims requiring proof are flagged.
