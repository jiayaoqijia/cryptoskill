# Team, Organization and Governance

Purpose: assess execution capacity, alignment and control. A good asset with a weak team is a bad investment; a strong team with bad incentives is worse.

## Founder and team

- Founders, roles, background verification, prior exits/failures, references.
- Critical roles: CTO, security, growth, finance, operations; identify gaps and single points of failure.
- Team size, hiring plan, retention, compensation, key-person dependence.
- Crypto: doxxed or pseudonymous; strength of identity evidence; prior projects including failed/abandoned ones; insider allocation and vesting.
- Do not infer competence from polished profiles; verify with primary sources.

## Funding and cap table

- Rounds, amounts, valuations, investors, dates; runway and burn; revenue versus funding.
- Cap table: founders, team, investors, treasury, options; dilution; liquidation preference; control.
- Related entities: subsidiaries, other projects, shared founders; conflicts and related-party transactions.
- For private companies: data room, customer references, contracts, churn, pipeline, use of proceeds.

## Governance

- Board, ownership, voting, veto, succession; disclosure quality.
- Crypto: owner hotkey, multisig, DAO/foundation; upgrade authority; treasury control; conviction locks.
- Are incentives aligned with long-term holders, or with emissions and short-term price?

## Tables

**Team map:** role, name, background, verified?, gaps.

**Funding/cap table:** round, amount, valuation, investor, date, dilution.

**Related parties:** entity, relationship, transaction, conflict, mitigation.

## Sources

- LinkedIn, GitHub, filings, cap table/data room, customer references, prior-project post-mortems.
- Verify biographies against at least one independent source; mark unverifiable claims.

## Red flags

- Unknown funding/runway; high burn; dependence on token sales.
- Founder spread across multiple projects; no succession; key-person concentration.
- Anonymous team with no legal entity; no references; inconsistent biographies.
- Insider allocation >20% with short vesting; related-party revenue.
- Governance decorative; owner can change rules unilaterally.

## Reporting

- Standard: one paragraph plus a table. Deep: full section with funding, cap table and related parties.
- Separate verified facts from claims; mark references not checked.
- State how anonymity or key-person risk changes the gate or confidence.
