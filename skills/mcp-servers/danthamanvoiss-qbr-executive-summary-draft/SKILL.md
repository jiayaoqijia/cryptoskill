## Purpose

Draft executive-ready QBR summary that aligns outcomes, risks, and next-quarter priorities.

## Use when

- Preparing a quarterly business review for leadership stakeholders.
- You need concise decision-level narrative from supplied reporting inputs.

## Required inputs

- Quarterly KPI summary and milestone updates [client-provided].
- Strategic goals and constraints from context/BRAND-CONTEXT.template.md and context/CLIENT-CONTEXT.template.md.
- Key risks, dependencies, and upcoming quarter priorities.

## Safety/authority

- No fabricated KPI changes, attributions, or forecast guarantees.
- Separate confirmed performance from interpretation/hypothesis.
- Draft-only executive summary, not a finalized client communication.

## Workflow

1. Summarize quarter outcomes against agreed objectives.
2. Highlight key wins, misses, and strategic implications.
3. Draft forward-looking priorities with assumptions and risks.
4. Add decision asks and unresolved questions.

## Output format
```

- Executive summary
- Outcomes vs objectives
- Forward-quarter priorities
- Decision asks and open risks
```

## Quality checks

- Narrative ties directly to supplied data and stated objectives.
- Unknowns and confidence qualifiers appear before recommendations.
