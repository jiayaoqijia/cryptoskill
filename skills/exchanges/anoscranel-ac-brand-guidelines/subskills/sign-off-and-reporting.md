# Sign-Off and Reporting

## Standard

- **ISO 24495-1 Plain Language Principles**: governance standards for validating document clarity and certifying compliance against established benchmarks. <https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html>
- **GOV.UK Publishing Standards**: operational release gating criteria ensuring content meets editorial quality before public deployment. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide>
- **Microsoft Writing Style Guide**: enterprise documentation governance and release sign-off protocols. <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/>
- **AP Stylebook**: professional editorial standards for maintaining accountability records and sign-off ledgers. <https://www.apstylebook.com/>
- ⚠️ House convention: any unresolved blocker-class finding prevents release sign-off; conditional approvals are strictly prohibited for critical brand non-compliance.

## Decision rules

1. Enforce binary release gates: documents pass only when zero blocker or major findings remain unresolved in the audit ledger.
2. Compile longitudinal compliance reports tracking finding frequency and severity trends across successive audit cycles.
3. Record formal sign-off metadata including author name, reviewer name, timestamp, and active specification version number.
4. Establish a mandatory re-audit cadence (monthly or quarterly) to prevent regression and terminology drift over time.

## Procedure

1. Review post-remediation audit verification ledgers to confirm all blocker and major findings are marked resolved.
2. Evaluate minor findings and suggestions, determining whether any require scheduled follow-up or can be accepted as residual debt.
3. Calculate final compliance scores and generate the executive compliance report summarizing corpus health trends.
4. Issue the formal pass or fail release gate decision based on strict adherence to the binary compliance threshold.
5. Record sign-off credentials in the governance ledger, capturing auditor ID, reviewer ID, timestamp, and spec version.
6. Archive the complete audit package, including specifications, checklists, ledgers, and final reports.
7. Schedule the next recurring re-audit date in the institutional calendar to ensure sustained compliance.

## Anti-patterns

- Granting release sign-off while blocker or major findings remain active in the remediation ledger.
- Issuing vague verbal approvals without recording formal sign-off metadata and specification versions.
- Failing to track longitudinal compliance trends, allowing recurring editorial defects to escape detection.
- Omitting scheduled re-audit cadences, leading to unmonitored terminology drift and brand degradation.

## Done checklist

- [ ] Post-remediation verification ledgers are fully reviewed.
- [ ] Blocker and major findings are confirmed resolved.
- [ ] Longitudinal compliance trends are calculated and reported.
- [ ] Formal pass/fail release gate decision is issued.
- [ ] Sign-off metadata (author, reviewer, timestamp, spec version) is recorded.
- [ ] Audit package and reports are properly archived.
- [ ] Recurring re-audit cadence is scheduled in the calendar.
