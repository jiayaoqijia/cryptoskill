## Description: <br>
Query Polymarket prediction market odds and events via CLI, including market search, current prices, and active events across sports, politics, crypto, elections, and geopolitics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[deanpress](https://clawhub.ai/user/deanpress) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to look up Polymarket market-implied probabilities, active events, prices, and order book snapshots as one input when assessing odds or prediction-market signals. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Market prices may be mistaken for financial advice or guaranteed predictions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat returned prices as market-implied probabilities and use them as one source among others. <br>
Risk: Search queries sent to Polymarket public endpoints may include sensitive private topics. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive personal, confidential, or private subjects as search terms. <br>
