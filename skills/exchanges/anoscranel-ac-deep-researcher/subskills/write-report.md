# Write the evidence report

## Standard

- Answer first. The verdict opens the report; the evidence follows. A reader
 who stops after paragraph one still has the answer.
- State the thesis under test and the verdict up front, the shape a
 gold exemplar uses.
- Every number names its primary source. A number without its primary is
 gossip with decimals.
- Population labels travel with numbers to the end. A benchmark figure arrives
 with what it measured, on whom, or not at all.
- Caveats are never stripped for brevity: ⚠️ flags, single-source labels,
 costs and rate limits survive every summary.
- Close with honest bounds: what the study did not establish, said plainly. Then refresh targets: the entities, numbers and
 hypotheses a future update re-checks.
- The chat reply is a five-line summary plus file paths, never the report
 pasted inline. Depth lives in files.

## Decision rules

- The deliverable shape chosen at scoping sets the section skeleton: verdict
 memo, landscape map, literature map, or evidence report.
- A refuted hypothesis gets a section that says so. Silence about a tested
 hypothesis is a lie of omission.
- If evidence balanced, the verdict says balanced. Forced certainty is a
 defect, not a style.

## Procedure

1. Open with the verdict and the thesis it answers.
2. One section per finding, each with confidence capped by rung.
3. A numbers table where every row names its primary.
4. The honest-bounds section.
5. The refresh-targets list.
6. The stop reason, verbatim from the stop decision.
7. Reply in chat with five lines or fewer plus the file paths.

## Anti-patterns

- The verdict buried at the end.
- Full sections pasted into the chat reply.
- Numbers stripped of primaries or populations in the final polish.
- A report that never states what it failed to establish.

## Done checklist

- Paragraph one holds the verdict.
- Every number traces to a named primary.
- Honest bounds and refresh targets exist.
- The stop reason appears verbatim.
- The chat reply is five lines or fewer plus paths.

The report's first paragraph carries the finding and its confidence, and everything after it is support; a document that builds to the conclusion has asked the reader to hold the whole argument before learning what it is for. Answer-first is a courtesy to the reader and a discipline on the writer.
