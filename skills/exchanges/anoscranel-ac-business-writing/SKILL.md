# Business Writing

## Overview

Two leaves cover the daily document load. email.md writes the one-ask email: subject, first line, ask, reply-ready close. memo-report.md writes the standalone internal document: memos, status reports, executive summaries, updates.

## When to use

- Drafting or replying to an email that needs one clear ask.
- Following up on a thread without nagging.
- Writing a memo, a report or an executive summary a decision-maker will read alone.

## When not to use

- Triage or routing of an inbox — outside this skill.
- Scored bids and grant narratives — outside this skill.
- Press statements or news copy — outside this skill.

## Precedence

> The deepest loaded instruction governs its uniquely-claimed scope; every `scope:` string is claimed by exactly one file in this tree; no leaf may widen a prohibition declared by an ancestor — leaves narrow, never override upward.
