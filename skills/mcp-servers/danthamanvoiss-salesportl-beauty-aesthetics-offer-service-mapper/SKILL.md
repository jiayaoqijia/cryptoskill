## Purpose
Map beauty and aesthetics services offers/services to ICP demand, operational capacity, and profitability goals.

## Use when
- You are operating inside `SalesPortl-Beauty-Aesthetics-Skills-Suite`.
- You need beauty and aesthetics services execution quality for med spas, beauty clinics, salons, and aesthetics brands.
- You want repeatable, improving workflows that learn from each run via context + memory updates.

## Industry fit: micro-niche variants
- high-ticket aesthetics clinics
- membership/retention salons
- multi-location beauty brands

## Standard operating patterns (SOP)
- offer bundling and package ladders
- booking and no-show mitigation
- before/after proof and consent-safe content

## SalesPortl platform tooling focus (if available in workspace)
- campaign flows for seasonal offers
- click tracking by service line
- calendar and rebooking workflow automations

## Required inputs
- Business objective and requested deliverable
- Current context quality (none/partial/verified)
- Business model, offer mix, and target customer profile
- Authority boundary for external actions (draft-only unless approved)
- Available SalesPortl platform features in this workspace

## Workflow
1. Map services/products by margin, demand profile, and delivery complexity.
2. Align offers to ICP segments and buying triggers specific to this industry.
3. Define package ladder and cross-sell/upsell pathways.
4. Specify proof requirements and measurable outcomes for each offer.
