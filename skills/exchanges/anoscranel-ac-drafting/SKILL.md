# Drafting

## Overview

Three leaves cover the start of a document: brief, outline, draft. The brief fixes the purpose, the reader and the success test. The outline turns the brief into an answer-first order with action headings. Drafting fills that order with the first full text. Everything here runs before any revision.

## When to use

- Briefing a new document: purpose, reader, venue rules, success test.
- Ordering a document: answer-first sequence, headings that carry the answer.
- Getting a first draft down, or turning rough notes into prose.

## When not to use

- The draft exists and needs restructuring, line tightening, a consistency pass or a final check — outside this skill.
- Finding, judging or citing sources — outside this skill.
- Picking the base style guide or shaping voice and tone — outside this skill.
